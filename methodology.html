Our Methodology

We deliver evidence with full nuance, honest uncertainty, and practical implications—in a format you can absorb during your commute.

Every episode answers one question clearly enough that you could teach it to someone else.

Evidence Standards

  • Every claim sourced — study, sample size, methodology
  • Correlation vs. causation — we never blur that line
  • Contradictions shown — both sides when studies disagree
  • Uncertainty stated — weak evidence labeled as such
  • Specific parameters — "90-120 min after waking" not "in the morning"
  • Natural citations — "A 2023 Nature meta-analysis found..."
  • Fact-checkable — verify against our sources

Episode Structure

Foundation: The "Why"

Key concepts, methodology, and terms explained with clear analogies. Technical terms always spelled out on first use (e.g., "polyunsaturated fatty acids, or PUFA").

Evidence: The "What"

Key studies cited with institution, year, and sample size. Conflicting evidence addressed directly—not hidden.

Application: The "How"

Protocols with exact parameters: timing, frequency, dosage. No vague advice—only actionable specifics.
