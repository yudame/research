## ElevenLabs Optimization Criteria

- Clear consonants and natural breathing
- Emotional variety (wistful, wry, matter-of-fact, emphatic)
- Varied pacing (contemplative to energized)
- Full sentences, no mid-thought cutoffs
- Characteristic Herzog cadence markers
