## Verified Sources by Tier

### Tier 1: Meta-analyses, Systematic Reviews, Official Statistics

**Saturated Fat:**
- Hooper L, et al. Reduction in saturated fat intake for cardiovascular disease. Cochrane Database Syst Rev. 2020. (15 RCTs, ~59,000 participants)
- Japanese systematic review and meta-analysis. 2024. (9 RCTs, 13,532 participants)
- Siri-Tarino PW, et al. Meta-analysis of prospective cohort studies evaluating the association of saturated fat with cardiovascular disease. Am J Clin Nutr. 2010. (21 prospective studies)
- PubMed review on saturated fat restriction for CVD prevention. April 2025.

**Added Sugar & CVD:**
- Yang Q, et al. Added sugar intake and cardiovascular diseases mortality among US adults. JAMA Intern Med. 2014. (US nationally representative sample)
- Te Morenga LA, et al. Dietary sugars and cardiometabolic risk: systematic review and meta-analyses. BMJ. 2013.
- Meta-analysis of total sugars, fructose, and sucrose with CVD mortality from prospective cohorts.

**Ultra-Processed Foods:**
- Hall KD, et al. Ultra-processed diets cause excess calorie intake and weight gain. Cell Metab. 2019. (Randomized crossover trial)
- American Heart Association guidance on ultra-processed foods. 2021.

**Microplastics:**
- Marfella R, et al. Microplastics and nanoplastics in atheromas and cardiovascular events. N Engl J Med. 2024 Mar. (257 patients, prospective observational)
- Leslie HA, et al. Discovery and quantification of plastic particle pollution in human blood. Environ Int. 2022.
- Detection of microplastics in human cardiac tissues. Environ Sci Technol. 2023. (9 microplastic types)

**Dietary Cholesterol:**
- Carson JAS, et al. Dietary cholesterol and cardiovascular risk: AHA scientific advisory. Circulation. 2020.
- Meta-regression analysis of 55 randomized dietary intervention studies on cholesterol.
- Meta-analysis of 28 RCTs on egg consumption and lipid profiles.

**Food Matrix:**
- Thorning TK, et al. Whole dairy matrix or single nutrients in assessment of health effects. Am J Clin Nutr. 2017. (Cheese vs. butter study)
- Systematic review comparing lycopene from whole tomato products vs. supplements for CVD risk factors.
- Meta-analysis of polyphenol biomarkers (8 studies; enterolactone and CVD/all-cause mortality).

**Mediterranean & Plant-Based Diets:**
- Umbrella review of 18 meta-analyses on Mediterranean diet and CVD outcomes.
- Orlich MJ, et al. Vegetarian dietary patterns and mortality. Seventh-day Adventist Health Study. JAMA Intern Med. 2013.
- Crowe FL, et al. Risk of ischemic heart disease in vegetarians and vegans. EPIC-Oxford. Am J Clin Nutr. 2013. (>2,820 IHD cases)

**Sodium & Blood Pressure:**
- Dose-response meta-analysis of 85 clinical trials (sodium intake 0.4-7.6 g/day).
- He FJ, MacGregor GA. A comprehensive review on salt and health and current experience of worldwide salt reduction programmes. J Hum Hypertens. 2009.

**Minerals:**
- Meta-analysis of magnesium supplementation in normotensive populations.
- Meta-analysis of potassium supplementation and blood pressure.

**Beverages:**
- Meta-analysis of wine consumption and CHD, CVD, CVD mortality.
- Meta-analysis of 84 prospective cohort studies on alcohol and cardiovascular disease.
- Meta-analysis on fruit juice consumption and mortality.

**Breakfast & Meal Timing:**
- Meta-analysis of prospective cohort studies on breakfast skipping and CVD/all-cause mortality.
- Network meta-analysis of 56 studies on intermittent fasting methods and cardiometabolic outcomes.

**Personalized Nutrition:**
- Celis-Morales C, et al. Effect of personalized nutrition on health-related behaviour: Food4Me trial. Int J Epidemiol. 2017. (N=1,607, 7 European countries)
- LIPGENE study: dietary fat quality and metabolic syndrome. 472 participants, 8 European countries.
- BMC Cardiovascular Disorders systematic review on gene-diet interactions. 2022.

### Tier 2: RCTs, Large Studies, Government Reports

**Saturated Fat:**
- Dehghan M, et al. Associations of fats and carbohydrate intake with cardiovascular disease and mortality (PURE study). Lancet. 2017. (135,335 participants, 18 countries)
- Ramsden CE, et al. Re-evaluation of the traditional diet-heart hypothesis: analysis of recovered data from Minnesota Coronary Experiment. BMJ. 2016.
- DELTA study (normolipidemic population, APOE genotype).

**Ultra-Processed Foods:**
- Framingham Offspring Study (18-year follow-up on UPF consumption and CVD).
- French NutriNet-Santé cohort study (UPF and CVD risk).

**Microplastics:**
- UC Riverside study on microplastics in arteries and atherosclerosis. November 2025.
- American Heart Association report on microplastics in carotid plaques of stroke survivors. April 2025.

**Mediterranean & Plant-Based Diets:**
- Estruch R, et al. Primary prevention of cardiovascular disease with Mediterranean diet (PREDIMED). N Engl J Med. 2018. (~4,500 participants)
- Delgado-Lista J, et al. Long-term secondary prevention of cardiovascular disease with Mediterranean diet (CORDIOPREV). Lancet. 2022.
- Twin study on Mediterranean diet adherence and IL-6 levels.

**Beverages:**
- Groh M, et al. Coffee consumption and risk of atrial fibrillation in patients after ablation (DECAF trial). JACC Clin Electrophysiol. 2024.
- PREDIMED analysis on moderate wine consumption and CV benefits. December 2024.

**Policy Interventions:**
- Mexico sugar-sweetened beverage tax modeling study (projected outcomes on diabetes and CVD). 2014-2024 data.
- Scarborough P, et al. Impact of UK Soft Drinks Industry Levy on purchases and childhood obesity. BMJ. 2020; updated 2022.
- South Africa Health Promotion Levy modeling study (stroke and mortality projections).
- Puska P, et al. The North Karelia Project: 30-year follow-up. Scand Cardiovasc J. 2009. (82-84% CVD mortality decline)
- Danish trans fat ban evaluation studies. 2004-2020.

**Dietary Guidelines:**
- 2020-2025 Dietary Guidelines for Americans. USDA/HHS.
- Scientific Report of the 2025 Dietary Guidelines Advisory Committee. December 2024.
- American Heart Association 2021 Dietary Guidance for Cardiovascular Health.
- European Society of Cardiology 2021 Prevention Guidelines.
- Nordic Nutrition Recommendations 2023.

**Regulations:**
- EU Regulation 2025/351 on plastic materials for food contact. Effective March 16, 2025.
- Health Canada FOP nutrition symbol regulations. Effective January 1, 2026.
- Colombia Law 2120 ("Junk Food Law") and Resolution 2492 of 2022. Mandatory warning labels.
- California Senate Bill 1147 (microplastics in drinking water study). Signed September 2024.
- California Assembly Bill 1200 (PFAS ban in food packaging). Effective January 2023.
- FDA Draft Guidance for Industry: Voluntary Sodium Reduction Goals (Phase II). August 2024.
- FDA Final Rule on "healthy" nutrient content claim. Effective February 25, 2025; compliance 2028.

**Personalized Nutrition:**
- PREDICT studies (ZOE/King's College London). 2024 Nature Medicine trial on personalized dietary programs.
- Berry SE, et al. Human postprandial responses to food and potential for precision nutrition. Nat Med. 2020.
- Study on cholesterol hyper-responders and dietary cholesterol vs. saturated fat. Am J Clin Nutr. 2025.
