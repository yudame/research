## Research Topic

Deep investigation into the relationship between diet and cardiovascular health, focusing on evidence beyond general dietary patterns. This episode builds on Episode 1's Mediterranean/DASH diet overview to explore specific dietary factors with nuanced evidence.

## Key Questions

- What does the current evidence show about saturated fat and cardiovascular outcomes? How do RCT findings compare to observational data?
- How do ultra-processed foods affect cardiovascular risk independently of their nutrient content?
- What is the dose-response relationship between added sugar consumption and cardiovascular disease?
- What does emerging research on microplastics and nanoplastics in the cardiovascular system reveal?
- How important is dietary cholesterol compared to saturated fat for cardiovascular health?
- What does the evidence suggest about food sources vs. isolated nutrients (the "food matrix" concept)?

## Context

Target audience: 40-year-old men interested in evidence-based cardiovascular health optimization.

Previous episodes covered:
- Ep. 1: Lifestyle foundations (including Mediterranean/DASH diet overview)
- Ep. 2: VO2 max
- Ep. 3: Heart rate variability
- Ep. 4: Supplementation

This episode goes deeper into specific dietary factors, examining areas of genuine scientific uncertainty and debate.

## Research Priorities

1. Systematic reviews and meta-analyses over individual studies
2. Effect sizes and practical significance
3. Study populations and generalizability to healthy middle-aged men
4. Contradictory findings and areas of genuine scientific uncertainty
5. Correlation vs. causation distinctions
6. Funding sources and potential conflicts of interest
