Cardiovascular Health

Evidence-based optimization of heart health, fitness, and longevity.

Apple Podcasts Spotify Other

Episodes

Ep 1 Lifestyle Foundations Full Report
How VO2 max and HRV predict longevity better than traditional risk factors, plus the lifestyle framework to optimize both.
More
Each 1-MET improvement in VO2 max reduces all-cause mortality by 11-17%, while optimal sleep and the Mediterranean diet create synergistic effects that can prevent over 80% of cardiovascular events.
Ep 2 VO2 Max Full Report
Evidence-based strategies to maximize VO2 max through polarized training, optimal intervals, and nutrition.
More
Deep dive into the strongest predictor of longevity through polarized training (80% easy, 20% brutally hard), optimal interval protocols like the 4x4 method, performance nutrition, and strategic supplementation.
Ep 3 Heart Rate Variability Full Report
Which HRV metrics matter, how to measure reliably, and which interventions show the largest effect sizes.
More
HRV offers a window into autonomic nervous system function and training readiness. Learn which metrics actually matter (RMSSD vs SDNN), how to measure reliably, when a 15% drop signals overtraining, and which interventions show the largest effect sizes.
Ep 4 Supplementation Full Report
What works, what fails spectacularly, and optimal dosing protocols for cardiovascular supplements.
More
Why high-dose omega-3 reduces heart attacks by 25% but increases atrial fibrillation risk by 50%, how CoQ10 cut heart failure mortality by 49%, and why vitamin D and niacin failed despite decades of hype.
Ep 5 Diet Coming Soon
Deep dive into dietary factors: the saturated fat controversy, ultra-processed foods, added sugar, and microplastics in cardiovascular tissue.
More
Examines how RCT findings on saturated fat differ from observational data, why ultra-processed foods may be an independent risk factor beyond their nutrient content, the dose-response relationship of added sugar, and the emerging research on microplastics and nanoplastics found in atherosclerotic plaques.
Ep 6 Beyond Lifestyle Basics Coming Soon
Non-traditional lifestyle factors: meditation, breathwork, social connection, sauna therapy, and more.
More
Explores the evidence for meditation and NSDR, breathing exercises for blood pressure and HRV, social isolation as a cardiovascular risk factor, sauna/heat therapy, cold exposure, nature exposure, purpose and spirituality, and how chronic stress compares to traditional risk factors.
