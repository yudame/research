 Now this is where the data becomes action. It is called HRV guided training and studies show it can lead to better performance gains than a rigid pre-plan schedule. The system is simple. Green light. Your HRV is normal or high. You feel good. Go for your planned high intensity workout. Yellow light. Your HRV is moderately suppressed.
