## Phase 2: Question Discovery

**After analyzing Perplexity's academic research, here are the questions we should investigate:**

### What subtopics and themes emerged?
- **Mind-body interventions:** Meditation, mindfulness, NSDR, breathing exercises - well covered with meta-analyses
- **Social determinants:** Loneliness/isolation effects comparable to smoking risk magnitude (29-32% increased CVD risk)
- **Environmental factors:** Green/blue space, air pollution, noise - strong epidemiological evidence
- **Occupational/circadian:** Job strain (10-40% excess risk), night shift work, light at night exposure
- **Temperature therapies:** Sauna (Finnish studies), cold exposure - more cautionary than expected
- **Psychological factors:** Purpose in life (17% risk reduction), spirituality, optimism, gratitude, forgiveness
- **Behavioral factors:** Pet ownership (24-31% mortality reduction), volunteering, cultural engagement, laughter

### What gaps exist in the academic literature?
- Limited RCT data on NSDR specifically (only one pilot study cited)
- Cold exposure evidence more mixed/cautionary than popular media suggests (Wim Hof study showed no significant effects)
- Mechanisms for cultural engagement protection not well understood
- Most studies in diseased populations or older adults - limited data specific to healthy middle-aged men
- Long-term intervention data sparse for gratitude, forgiveness, awe experiences
- Dose-response relationships unclear for most factors (how much is enough?)

### What recent developments aren't covered?
- Latest 2024-2025 research on any of these topics
- Post-COVID research on social isolation effects (pandemic impact)
- Emerging wearable technology and HRV monitoring applications
- Digital interventions for social connection and mind-body practices
- Recent guidelines or position statements from cardiology societies

### What contradictions or uncertainties need more sources?
- Cold exposure: Popular claims vs. limited scientific evidence (Wim Hof study was negative)
- Sauna: Strong observational data (Finnish studies) but RCT evidence "does not support" most benefits
- Meditation: Effect sizes vary widely across studies (heterogeneity noted)
- Sex differences: Purpose/spirituality protective in men but not women - why?
- Breathing exercise protocols: Optimal technique, frequency, duration unclear

### What industry/implementation questions arose?
- Specific protocols recommended by practitioners (breathing rate, sauna temp/duration)
- Which meditation apps/programs have clinical evidence?
- How do cardiologists integrate these factors into practice?
- Patient adherence rates and barriers to these interventions
- Cost-effectiveness of mind-body programs vs. medications

### What policy/regulatory angles need investigation?
- Healthcare/insurance coverage for mind-body interventions
- Occupational health policies around shift work and circadian disruption
- Urban planning guidelines for green space access
- Workplace wellness program regulations and effectiveness

### What practitioner perspectives are missing?
- What are cardiologists actually recommending to patients?
- Integration with cardiac rehabilitation programs
- Real-world implementation challenges
- Patient preferences and willingness to adopt these practices
