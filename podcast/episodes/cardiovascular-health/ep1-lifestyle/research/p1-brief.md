## Research Topic

Foundational framework for understanding how VO2 max and HRV predict longevity, and how the "big three" lifestyle factors (sleep, exercise basics, nutrition) synergistically affect cardiovascular health in middle-aged adults.

## Series Context - Critical Scope Boundaries

This is Episode 1 of a 6-part series. Later episodes go deep on specific topics:

| Episode | Topic | What Ep 1 Should NOT Cover |
|---------|-------|---------------------------|
| **Ep 2** | VO2 Max Training | Polarized training, 4x4 intervals, intensity distribution, detraining |
| **Ep 3** | HRV | RMSSD vs SDNN metrics, measurement devices, overtraining detection |
| **Ep 4** | Supplementation | Omega-3, CoQ10, specific supplement protocols |
| **Ep 5** | Diet Deep Dive | Saturated fat controversy, ultra-processed foods, microplastics |
| **Ep 6** | Beyond Lifestyle | Meditation, sauna, cold exposure, social connection, breathing |

**Episode 1 should establish the "WHY" and foundations, not the "HOW" specifics.**

## Key Questions for Episode 1

1. **What are VO2 max and HRV at a conceptual level?** Why are they stronger predictors of longevity than traditional risk factors? (High-level definitions only - Ep 2 and 3 go deep)

2. **What is the mortality-fitness relationship?** Each 1-MET increase = X% mortality reduction. What does the research show about elite fitness and longevity?

3. **How does sleep affect cardiovascular health?** Optimal duration (U-shaped curve), quality effects on HRV, sleep's role in recovery and adaptation.

4. **What are the fundamentals of exercise for heart health?** Volume/frequency basics for cardiovascular benefit. (NOT specific protocols - that's Ep 2)

5. **What do Mediterranean and DASH diets show for heart health?** Overview of dietary patterns and cardiovascular outcomes. (NOT detailed controversies - that's Ep 5)

6. **How do lifestyle factors interact synergistically?** The multiplier effects when sleep, exercise, and nutrition work together. Can prevent 80%+ of cardiovascular events.

Intelligent, health-conscious adults (especially 40+ males) with:
- ~10 hours/week exercise capacity
- 8 hours/night sleep
- Interest in understanding the evidence base for cardiovascular optimization
