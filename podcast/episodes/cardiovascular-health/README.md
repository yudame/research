# Cardiovascular Health Series

A 4-part evidence-based podcast series exploring the science of heart health, fitness optimization, and longevity through the lens of research methodology and critical analysis.

## Series Overview

This series examines cardiovascular health not through prescriptive advice, but through rigorous evaluation of the evidence. Each episode follows the research methodology principles outlined in the Yudame Research framework: prioritizing peer-reviewed studies, distinguishing correlation from causation, reporting effect sizes, noting study populations, and highlighting contradictory findings.

### Episode Arc

**Episode 1: Lifestyle Foundations**
Establishes the foundational framework by examining how VO2 max and heart rate variability (HRV) predict longevity better than traditional risk factors. Explores the lifestyle interventions—sleep, exercise, and Mediterranean diet—that create synergistic effects capable of preventing over 80% of cardiovascular events. Each 1-MET improvement in VO2 max reduces all-cause mortality by 11-17%.

**Episode 2: VO2 Max**
Deep dive into maximizing VO2 max, the strongest single predictor of longevity. Examines polarized training (80% easy, 20% brutally hard), optimal interval protocols like the 4x4 method, performance nutrition, and strategic supplementation. Analyzes the evidence for why intensity matters more than volume for VO2 max improvement.

**Episode 3: Heart Rate Variability**
Explores HRV as a window into autonomic nervous system function and training readiness. Distinguishes which metrics matter (RMSSD vs SDNN), how to measure reliably with consumer wearables, when a 15% drop signals overtraining, and which interventions show the largest effect sizes. Evaluates the gap between clinical applications and consumer device capabilities.

**Episode 4: Supplementation**
Evidence-based analysis of cardiovascular supplements and medications: what works, what fails spectacularly, and optimal dosing protocols. Examines why high-dose omega-3 reduces heart attacks by 25% but increases atrial fibrillation risk by 50%, how CoQ10 cut heart failure mortality by 49%, and why vitamin D and niacin failed despite decades of hype.

## Research Methodology

Each episode was developed using:
- **ChatGPT Deep Research** - Industry analysis and case studies
- **Perplexity Deep Research** - Academic foundation and peer-reviewed literature
- **NotebookLM** - Audio synthesis from research reports

### Key Principles Applied

1. **Prioritize systematic reviews and meta-analyses** over individual studies
2. **Report effect sizes** and practical significance, not just p-values
3. **Note study populations** and generalizability limitations
4. **Compare individual studies against meta-analyses** to identify outliers
5. **Distinguish preliminary vs well-replicated findings**
6. **Include contradictory findings and uncertainties**
7. **Note funding sources and conflicts of interest**

## Research Transparency

All research materials, including ChatGPT and Perplexity outputs, source lists, and review notes, are included in each episode's `research/` directory. This allows listeners to:
- Verify claims against original sources
- Understand the research process
- Identify gaps or biases in the synthesis
- Build on the foundation for their own exploration
