# Research Brief: Stablecoin Series - Ep. 5, Reserve Management & Custody Infrastructure

**Date:** 2025-12-26
**Episode:** Stablecoin Series - Ep. 5, Reserve Management & Custody Infrastructure

---

## Research Topic

How do stablecoin issuers safely hold and manage the assets backing their tokens, and what infrastructure is required to meet regulatory standards (GENIUS Act, MiCA, Singapore MAS) while maintaining operational efficiency and user confidence?

## Key Questions

1. **Reserve Composition**: What assets are permitted under different regulatory frameworks, and how do asset choices affect liquidity, credit risk, and yield?

2. **Custody Infrastructure**: What makes a custodian "qualified" and how do custody arrangements differ between fiat reserves and crypto collateral?

3. **Attestation Requirements**: What do AICPA 2025 Criteria require, how do attestations differ from full audits, and what are compliance costs?

4. **Liquidity & Redemption**: How do issuers manage the tension between liquid reserves and yield generation, and what happens during bank runs?

5. **Risk Management**: What risks exist (credit, custodian insolvency, operational), what insurance is available, and how do bankruptcy-remote structures work?

6. **Transparency Standards**: What reporting practices have major stablecoins adopted, and what level of detail satisfies both regulators and users?

## Context

This is Episode 5 in a 6-part series on stablecoins. Previous episodes covered:
- Ep. 1: Market landscape and why stablecoins matter
- Ep. 2: Regulatory frameworks (GENIUS Act, MiCA, global approaches)
- Ep. 3: Token economics and maintaining the peg
- Ep. 4: Technical architecture (blockchain infrastructure)

Episode 5 focuses on the operational and compliance infrastructure that makes stablecoin reserves trustworthy. Key case studies include Circle's SVB crisis, Tether's reserve composition changes, and emerging transparency standards from RLUSD.
