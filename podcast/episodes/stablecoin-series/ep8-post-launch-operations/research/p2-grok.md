### Stablecoin Operations in Early 2026: Regulatory Momentum and Practical Hurdles

The landscape of stablecoin post-launch operations in January 2026 reflects a sector transitioning from regulatory uncertainty to structured implementation, driven largely by the GENIUS Act's phased rollout. Enacted in July 2025, this landmark legislation defines payment stablecoins as neither securities nor commodities, mandating issuance by regulated entities like bank subsidiaries or OCC-licensed non-banks, with strict 1:1 reserve backing using high-quality assets such as U.S. Treasury securities or demand deposits. Reserves cannot be rehypothecated beyond limited purposes, and issuers must disclose compositions monthly while prohibiting interest or yield payments to avoid deposit-like features. This framework aims to integrate stablecoins into mainstream finance, but early discussions reveal tensions around compliance costs, interoperability, and illicit finance risks.
