# Research Brief: Stablecoin Token Economic Design & Stabilization Mechanisms

**Date:** 2025-12-26
**Episode:** Stablecoin Series: Ep. 3, Token Economic Design & Stabilization Mechanisms

---

## Research Topic

This episode investigates the economic architecture underlying stablecoins, comparing different stabilization mechanisms, collateralization models, and monetary policy approaches. We analyze real-world performance data to understand which designs have proven resilient and which have failed.

## Key Questions

- What collateralization model provides the best balance of stability, capital efficiency, and user trust?
- Can algorithmic stablecoins achieve long-term stability without external collateral?
- What does the Terra/UST collapse reveal about fundamental design flaws in algorithmic models?
- How do different reserve compositions (cash, treasuries, commercial paper, crypto) affect risk profiles?
- What governance structures prevent value extraction while enabling necessary protocol changes?
- What revenue models can sustain stablecoin operations without relying on unsustainable subsidies?
- How have major stablecoins performed during stress events (Black Thursday 2020, Terra collapse 2022, banking crisis 2023)?

## Context

This is Episode 3 of a 3-part stablecoin series:
- Episode 1: Market structure and competitive dynamics
- Episode 2: Regulatory frameworks (GENIUS Act, MiCA, global approaches)
- **Episode 3 (this episode):** Token economics and stabilization mechanisms

The audience has foundational knowledge from the prior episodes. This episode dives deep into the technical and economic design choices that determine stablecoin stability and sustainability.
