# Research Brief: Stablecoin Series: Ep. 7, Go-to-Market Strategy & User Adoption

**Date:** 2025-12-26
**Episode:** Stablecoin Series: Ep. 7, Go-to-Market Strategy & User Adoption

---

## Research Topic

Go-to-market strategies for stablecoins, user adoption tactics, overcoming incumbent network effects (USDT/USDC dominance), value proposition development, target segment selection, and distribution channel analysis.

## Key Questions

1. What use cases drive stablecoin adoption and which segments show strongest growth?
2. How do successful stablecoins overcome the network effects of incumbents like USDT and USDC?
3. What value propositions resonate beyond simple cost savings (speed, programmability, accessibility)?
4. What partnerships and integrations are most critical for driving adoption?
5. How do trust-building strategies differ in a market scarred by failures (Terra, Iron Finance)?
6. What UX improvements are necessary to reach mainstream vs. crypto-native users?
7. Which geographic markets offer the best opportunities for new entrants in 2025?
8. How much of adoption is driven by genuine utility vs. incentives and speculation?

## Context

This is Episode 7 of the Stablecoin Series, focusing on go-to-market strategy after covering technical architecture (Ep. 4), reserve management (Ep. 5), and liquidity partnerships (Ep. 6). The research should provide evidence-based insights into what actually drives user adoption, drawing on transaction data, case studies (USDC growth, PayPal PYUSD), and market research from 2024-2025.

**Key areas to investigate:**
- Target segment selection (B2B cross-border, remittances, DeFi, consumer payments)
- Payment network integration (Visa/Mastercard partnerships, on/off-ramps)
- User experience barriers (wallet complexity, key management, gas fees, KYC friction)
- Trust building and brand strategy post-Terra
- Distribution channels (exchanges, DeFi protocols, merchant adoption)
- Geographic strategy (regional adoption patterns, regulatory environments)
