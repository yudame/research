# Episode 7: Go-to-Market Strategy & User Adoption

## Core Research Question

How do you drive actual usage of a stablecoin in a market dominated by incumbents, and what strategies have proven effective at overcoming adoption barriers?

## Research Objectives

Investigate go-to-market strategies, target segment selection, value proposition development, and user adoption tactics. Analyze why some stablecoins achieve widespread usage while others remain unused despite technical competence and adequate liquidity.

## Key Investigation Areas

### 1. Target Segment Selection
- What use cases drive stablecoin adoption (B2B cross-border payments, remittances, DeFi, consumer payments, store of value)?
- Which segments have shown strongest growth (empirical data on transaction volumes by use case)?
- How do different regions prioritize different use cases (e.g., Latin America remittances, Asia B2B trade, North America DeFi)?
- What does market research show about enterprise vs. retail user needs?
- How do different user segments evaluate stablecoins (trust, speed, cost, ease of use, regulatory status)?

### 2. Value Proposition Beyond Cost Savings
- What does 2025 data show about primary adoption drivers (speed vs. cost vs. accessibility)?
- How has the narrative shifted from "lower fees" to "real-time settlement"?
- What additional value do stablecoins provide (programmability, 24/7 availability, composability with DeFi)?
- How do value propositions differ by use case (merchant payments vs. treasury management vs. trading)?
- What does user research reveal about why people actually use stablecoins?

### 3. Payment Network Integration
- How do stablecoins integrate with traditional payment infrastructure (Visa, Mastercard partnerships)?
- What is the status of bank-issued stablecoins vs. fintech/crypto-native issuers?
- How do on-ramp and off-ramp solutions affect usability (fiat → stablecoin → fiat)?
- What payment processors and gateways support stablecoin acceptance?
- How does integration with existing financial systems affect adoption?

### 4. User Experience & Adoption Barriers
- What usability challenges prevent mainstream adoption (complex wallet addresses, key management, gas fees)?
- How are projects simplifying UX (username systems like Mastercard Crypto Credential, account abstraction, gasless transactions)?
- What does user research show about friction points in the conversion funnel?
- How do KYC/AML requirements affect user onboarding?
- What percentage of potential users abandon the process due to complexity?

### 5. Trust Building & Brand Strategy
- How do new stablecoins establish credibility in a market scarred by failures (Terra, Iron Finance)?
- What role does regulatory compliance play in building trust (GENIUS Act authorization, MiCA licensing)?
- How important is transparency (reserve reporting, audits) to user adoption?
- What does brand positioning look like (financial stability, innovation, compliance, decentralization)?
- How do partnerships (banks, payment networks, major crypto platforms) signal legitimacy?

### 6. Distribution Channels & Partnerships
- What distribution channels have proven most effective (exchange listings, DeFi protocol integrations, wallet defaults, merchant adoption)?
- How do platform partnerships accelerate adoption (e.g., Circle's integration with Coinbase)?
- What role do developer ecosystems play (SDKs, APIs, documentation)?
- How do B2B partnerships differ from B2C distribution?
- What incentive programs drive adoption (cashback, rewards, yield)?

### 7. Geographic Strategy & Market Selection
- Which markets are adopting stablecoins fastest (empirical data by region)?
- How do regulatory environments affect market entry decisions?
- What infrastructure prerequisites exist (banking relationships, local exchanges, payment networks)?
- How do macroeconomic conditions (currency instability, capital controls) affect adoption?
- What does data show about stablecoin usage in emerging vs. developed markets?

## Research Methodology

- **Analyze transaction data**: Examine actual usage patterns, volume growth, and user demographics
- **Study successful launches**: How did USDC grow despite Tether's dominance? What did PayPal's PYUSD strategy entail?
- **Compare adoption curves**: How quickly did different stablecoins reach milestones (1M users, $1B market cap)?
- **Survey user research**: What do surveys and studies reveal about adoption motivations and barriers?
- **Evaluate market data**: Which regions, use cases, and platforms show strongest growth?
- **Examine failed GTM strategies**: What approaches have not worked despite significant investment?
- **Track regulatory impacts**: How do GENIUS Act and MiCA affect market positioning?

## Key Questions to Answer

1. What use case(s) should a new stablecoin prioritize to gain initial traction?
2. Is the primary value proposition speed, cost, accessibility, or something else?
3. How do you overcome the network effects protecting USDT and USDC?
4. What partnerships are most critical for driving adoption?
5. How important is regulatory compliance vs. permissionless innovation to user trust?
6. What UX improvements are necessary to reach mainstream users vs. crypto-native users?
7. Which geographic markets offer the best opportunities for new entrants?
8. How much does marketing and incentives vs. organic utility drive adoption?

## Critical Success Factors to Evaluate

- **User growth rate**: Monthly active addresses, new user acquisition
- **Transaction volume**: Actual usage vs. speculative trading
- **Use case diversity**: Concentration in one use case vs. broad adoption
- **Geographic reach**: Single market vs. multi-regional adoption
- **Ecosystem integration**: Number of platforms, protocols, and partners supporting the stablecoin
- **Retention metrics**: One-time users vs. regular, recurring usage
- **Organic vs. incentivized usage**: Sustainable adoption vs. subsidy-dependent activity

## Sources to Prioritize

- Market research reports on stablecoin adoption (Fireblocks State of Stablecoins 2025, Artemis Stablecoin Payments report)
- Transaction data and on-chain analytics (unique addresses, volume by use case, geographic distribution)
- Case studies: USDC vs. Tether, PayPal's PYUSD launch, bank-issued stablecoins
- User surveys and adoption research
- Payment network partnership announcements (Visa, Mastercard)
- Regional adoption data (Latin America, Asia-Pacific, Africa)
- Academic research on cryptocurrency adoption and network effects
- Industry reports on payment innovation and cross-border transactions

## Approach to Uncertainty

- Distinguish between stated reasons for adoption and revealed preferences (what people say vs. what they do)
- Note that adoption drivers may differ significantly across regions and use cases
- Acknowledge that incentive programs can mask true product-market fit
- Recognize that regulatory clarity (or lack thereof) may retrospectively change adoption patterns
- Identify where claims about "mainstream adoption" are aspirational vs. empirical
- Report when network effects create winner-take-most dynamics vs. allowing coexistence
- Consider that macro conditions (bull/bear markets, economic crises) significantly affect adoption timing

## Output Goals

The research should provide evidence-based insights into:
- Which use cases and target segments have demonstrated strongest adoption
- What value propositions resonate most with different user types
- How successful stablecoins have overcome incumbents' network effects
- What partnerships and integrations accelerate vs. merely signal credibility
- Where user experience improvements have materially impacted adoption
- How regulatory positioning affects trust and market access
- What geographic markets present the best opportunities in 2025
- What the realistic path to meaningful adoption looks like for a new stablecoin
- How much of adoption is driven by genuine utility vs. speculation and incentives
