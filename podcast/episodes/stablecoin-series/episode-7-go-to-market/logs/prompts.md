### PERPLEXITY - Phase 1 Academic Foundation

**Focus:** Academic studies, meta-analyses, market research, user adoption research
**Duration:** 30-120 seconds (API-based)

```
Research stablecoin go-to-market strategies, user adoption patterns, and network effects in cryptocurrency markets. Focus on: (1) What use cases drive stablecoin adoption (cross-border payments, remittances, DeFi, consumer payments, store of value) and which segments show strongest 2024-2025 growth by transaction volume? (2) How have challenger stablecoins like USDC overcome incumbent network effects (Tether dominance)—what strategies worked and what didn't? (3) What value propositions beyond cost savings drive adoption (real-time settlement, 24/7 availability, programmability, composability)? (4) What does empirical research show about user adoption barriers (wallet complexity, key management, gas fees, KYC friction) and what percentage of potential users abandon onboarding? (5) How do payment network integrations (Visa, Mastercard partnerships) and on/off-ramp solutions affect mainstream adoption? (6) What does user research reveal about trust building post-Terra collapse—how do transparency, regulatory compliance, and brand positioning affect user willingness to adopt? (7) Which geographic markets show highest stablecoin adoption rates and why (Latin America remittances, Asia B2B trade, emerging market currency instability)? (8) What distinguishes organic utility-driven adoption from incentive/speculation-driven usage?

**Research methodology:**
- Prioritize peer-reviewed studies, meta-analyses, systematic reviews, and authoritative sources
- Distinguish between correlation and causation in findings
- Report effect sizes and practical significance, not just statistical significance
- Note the study populations and whether findings generalize to relevant demographics
- Compare individual studies against meta-analyses and systematic reviews
- Identify preliminary research vs. well-replicated findings
- Note funding sources and potential conflicts of interest when relevant
- Include contradictory findings and areas of scientific uncertainty
- Cite specific studies, researchers, and sources throughout
- Provide full source URLs for all citations

**Output:** Comprehensive research report with extensive citations, sample sizes, methodological details, and source links covering stablecoin adoption drivers, network effects, user experience barriers, geographic patterns, and trust-building strategies.
```

**Focus:** Cross-dimensional analysis, synthesis, verification of key claims

Research stablecoin adoption and go-to-market strategies, focusing on these specific questions that require multi-dimensional analysis:
