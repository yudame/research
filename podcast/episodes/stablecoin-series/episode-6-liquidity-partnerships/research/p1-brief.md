## Research Topic

How do you ensure a stablecoin is actually usable and tradable? Investigating market maker partnerships, exchange listings, DEX integrations, multi-chain liquidity, and what separates widely-adopted stablecoins from those that remain illiquid.

## Key Questions

1. What minimum liquidity is required for a stablecoin to function effectively?
2. Which partnerships are most critical (market makers, exchanges, DeFi protocols)?
3. How much does it cost to establish professional market making and exchange listings?
4. What differentiates successful liquidity strategies from failed attempts?
5. How do new entrants compete against USDT and USDC's network effects?
6. What role do liquidity incentives (yield, token rewards) play, and are they sustainable?
7. How does multi-chain deployment affect liquidity efficiency?

## Context

This is Episode 6 of the 8-part Stablecoin Series, focusing on the infrastructure required to make a stablecoin liquid and accessible. Previous episodes covered market evolution (Ep 1), legal compliance (Ep 2), token economics (Ep 3), technical architecture (Ep 4), and reserve management (Ep 5). This episode investigates market maker partnerships, exchange listings, DEX integrations, and multi-chain liquidity strategies.
