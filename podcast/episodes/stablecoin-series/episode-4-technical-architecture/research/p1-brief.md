# Research Brief: Stablecoin Technical Architecture & Smart Contract Development

**Date:** 2025-12-26
**Episode:** Stablecoin Series: Ep. 4, Technical Architecture & Smart Contract Development

---

## Research Topic

Investigate the technical architecture of stablecoins, from blockchain selection to smart contract design to security practices. Analyze real-world security incidents to understand vulnerabilities and identify best practices that have proven effective at scale.

## Key Questions

- Which blockchain(s) offer the best balance of security, cost, speed, and ecosystem compatibility for stablecoin deployment?
- What smart contract design patterns have proven most secure and reliable at scale?
- How effective are different audit methodologies at catching critical vulnerabilities?
- What key management practices are necessary to prevent insider threats and operational failures?
- How should projects balance upgradeability (fixing bugs) with immutability (preventing malicious changes)?
- What technical decisions are regulatory requirements (GENIUS Act, MiCA) beginning to mandate?

## Context

This is Episode 4 in the Stablecoin Series. Previous episodes covered:
- Ep 1: Market Evolution (market dynamics, major players)
- Ep 2: Legal & Compliance (regulatory frameworks, licensing)
- Ep 3: Token Economics (mechanisms, peg stability)

This episode focuses on the technical implementation layer - how stablecoins are actually built, what security practices matter, and what we can learn from historical exploits and successes.

## Investigation Areas

1. **Blockchain Selection & Multi-Chain Strategy** - Transaction speed, finality, cost, security, developer tools, bridge vulnerabilities
2. **Smart Contract Architecture** - Token standards, upgrade mechanisms, pausable contracts, access control
3. **Security Audit Process** - Automated vs manual review, formal verification, audit costs, bug bounties
4. **Common Vulnerabilities & Exploits** - Attack vectors, historical incidents, lessons learned
5. **Key Management & Operational Security** - Multi-sig, HSMs, threshold signing, ISO/IEC 27001
6. **Deployment Strategy & Testing** - Testing methodologies, rollback mechanisms, upgrade transparency
