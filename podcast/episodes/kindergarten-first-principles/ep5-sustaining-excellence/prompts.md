## Research Phase

### Research Prompt

**Tool Used:** [e.g., Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Grok, etc.]

**Prompt:**
```
Research teacher and caregiver wellbeing in early childhood education, particularly in intensive low-ratio or one-on-one settings (including homeschooling, micro-schools, and highly individualized care).

**Context:** This is for understanding the sustainability of high-quality early childhood education that requires intensive adult engagement. Focus on evidence that can inform both system design and individual practitioner support.

**Key research areas:**
- Burnout and emotional labor in early childhood settings
- Sustainability factors in intensive teaching/caregiving relationships
- Support structures that enable long-term effectiveness
- Professional isolation and community needs
- Professional development and growth pathways
- Ecological models of educator wellbeing (individual, organizational, systemic factors)
- Comparative research: traditional classrooms vs. low-ratio vs. one-on-one settings
- Recovery, restoration, and resilience strategies

**Research methodology:**
- Prioritize peer-reviewed studies, meta-analyses, and authoritative sources on educator wellbeing
- Distinguish between correlation and causation in findings (e.g., does support reduce burnout, or do resilient teachers seek out support?)
- Report effect sizes and practical significance, not just statistical significance
- Note the study populations (age groups taught, setting types, geographic regions) and whether findings generalize to intensive early childhood settings
- Compare individual studies against meta-analyses and systematic reviews
- Identify preliminary research vs. well-replicated findings in educator wellbeing research
- Note funding sources and potential conflicts of interest when relevant
- Include contradictory findings and areas of scientific uncertainty
- Examine both quantitative measures (burnout scales, retention rates) and qualitative insights (lived experiences)
- Look for longitudinal studies tracking educator wellbeing over time
- Consider both prevention strategies and intervention effectiveness
- Cite specific studies, researchers, and sources throughout

**Output:** Comprehensive research report with extensive citations and source links that can inform both individual practice and system design for sustainable excellence in early childhood education.
```
