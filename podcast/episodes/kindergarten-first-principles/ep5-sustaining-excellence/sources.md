## Key Meta-Analyses and Systematic Reviews

1. [Yin et al. (2017) - Meta-Analysis of Class Sizes and Ratios in Early Childhood Education](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/EJ1149537.pdf) - Establishes the threshold effects for ratios (7.5:1) and class sizes
2. [Haddock et al. (2017) - The Effectiveness of Interventions Aimed at Reducing Teacher Burnout](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10648-017-9420-8) - Meta-analysis showing overall intervention effect sizes
3. [Ng (2023) - A systematic review of burnout and quality of life of early childhood educators](https://www.iier.org.au/iier33/ng.pdf) - Comprehensive review of burnout prevalence and predictors
4. [AI-assisted systematic review (2024)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/369453000_A_systematic_review_of_burnout_and_quality_of_life_of_early_childhood_educators) - Develops four-domain wellbeing model

## Large-Scale Empirical Studies

5. [Wang et al. (2020) - Burnout among Chinese preschool teachers (N=1,795)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6998270/) - 53.2% burnout prevalence with detailed correlates
6. [Finnish recovery study (2020) - Recovery experiences and teacher wellbeing (N=909)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7007884/) - DRAMMA model showing psychological detachment effects
7. [Louisiana ECE longitudinal study - Frank Porter Graham analysis](https://fpg.unc.edu/news/investigating-teaching-staff-turnover-early-childhood-education) - 60% leave within 3 years

## Key Intervention Studies

8. CARE for Teachers RCT - Mindfulness intervention with coaching (36 schools, 224 teachers)
9. [Fostering Resilience study (2024) - Organizational climate effects](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/1350293X.2024.2391405)
10. [Early childhood special education burnout study (2022) - Job demands-resources model](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9640182/)
