# Research Prompt: Episode 3 - Infrastructure Without Capital

## Episode Context

**Series:** Solomon Islands Telecom Launch Series (Episode 3 of 6)

**Episode Title:** Infrastructure Without Capital - The SATSOL Advantage

**Previous Episode Context:**
- Episode 1: Established financial inclusion opportunity (75% unbanked, 80% cash economy, 70% mobile ownership)
- Episode 2: Analyzed duopoly market (Our Telekom vs Bmobile) and regulatory landscape for third-player entry

**This Episode Focus:** How partnering with SATSOL (established infrastructure provider with fiber, Starlink access, and enterprise relationships) solves the biggest barrier to telecom market entry—infrastructure capital and the 1,000-island geographic challenge—enabling faster, capital-efficient network deployment.

**Target Audience:** Infrastructure investors, telecommunications executives, development finance institutions, and network engineers interested in capital-efficient deployment strategies for challenging geographies.

---

## Research Objectives

Understand the infrastructure economics and technical architecture for launching mobile networks across archipelagos. Analyze how infrastructure partnerships (vs. build-from-scratch) change the capital requirements, deployment timeline, and operational model. Evaluate Starlink satellite backhaul compared to traditional submarine cable + microwave solutions, and assess how SATSOL's existing capabilities create competitive advantages for a new mobile network operator.

---

## Core Research Questions

### Infrastructure Economics

1. **What infrastructure investment is typically required to launch mobile networks across archipelagos?**
   - What are typical capital requirements for mobile network launches in Pacific island nations?
   - Breakdown: spectrum acquisition, tower deployment, backhaul infrastructure, core network equipment, power systems
   - How do costs scale across dispersed island geographies vs. concentrated urban markets?
   - What operational costs (backhaul bandwidth, site maintenance, power/fuel) dominate in island contexts?

2. **How do infrastructure partnerships (vs. build-from-scratch) change the economics and timeline of market entry?**
   - What models exist for infrastructure sharing or partnership in telecom (tower sharing, backhaul leasing, MVNO arrangements)?
   - How do capital requirements shift when leveraging existing infrastructure vs. building greenfield?
   - What timeline acceleration is possible with partnership vs. building from scratch (months/years)?
   - What are the trade-offs (capital savings vs. operational costs, flexibility vs. dependency)?

### Satellite vs. Submarine Cable Backhaul

3. **How does Starlink satellite backhaul compare to traditional submarine cable + microwave solutions for island connectivity?**
   - **Cost comparison:** Starlink terminal + service costs vs. submarine cable landing + microwave link buildout
   - **Performance comparison:** Latency, bandwidth, reliability, uptime in each approach
   - **Deployment speed:** Days (Starlink) vs. months/years (cable + microwave infrastructure)
   - **Scalability:** Adding new island sites with satellite vs. extending terrestrial/cable infrastructure
   - **Technical limitations:** Starlink capacity constraints, weather impacts, vs. cable vulnerability to damage
   - **Real-world examples:** Where has Starlink been used successfully for cellular backhaul in remote areas?

4. **What role does SATSOL's existing fiber network play in urban coverage (Honiara, provincial centers)?**
   - Where does SATSOL have fiber infrastructure deployed currently?
   - How does fiber backhaul enable higher-capacity urban networks vs. satellite/microwave?
   - What hybrid architecture combines fiber (urban) + Starlink (remote islands) optimally?

### Network Architecture

5. **How do you plan tower deployment across 85% rural population with SINBIP (Solomon Islands National Broadband Infrastructure Project) coming online?**
   - What is SINBIP: 161 tower sites by 2026—where are they located, who operates them?
   - Can new mobile operators access SINBIP infrastructure (infrastructure sharing regulations)?
   - How do you prioritize which islands/regions to cover first (population density, economic activity, government priorities)?
   - What technical approach works for low-density rural coverage (macro towers, small cells, distributed antenna systems)?

6. **What technical architecture balances fiber, Starlink, and cellular networks for optimal coverage/cost?**
   - Core network architecture: Where do mobile switching centers, packet core, IMS sit (cloud, on-premise, regional)?
   - Backhaul strategy: Fiber for urban, Starlink for remote, microwave for mid-range?
   - Spectrum strategy: What frequencies are optimal for island coverage (low-band 700MHz for range vs. mid-band for capacity)?
   - Network sharing: Can tower sites, backhaul, or spectrum be shared with partners or incumbents?

### Resilience and Climate

7. **How do you ensure network resilience in disaster-prone regions (cyclones, earthquakes, climate vulnerability)?**
   - What infrastructure hardening is required (tower reinforcement, backup power, weatherproofing)?
   - How does Starlink's quick deployment help with disaster recovery vs. damaged cable/terrestrial infrastructure?
   - What redundancy strategies work (diverse backhaul paths, distributed core network, battery backup)?
   - What examples exist from cyclone impacts on telecom infrastructure in Pacific (Fiji, Vanuatu, Tonga)?

### Regulatory and Operational

8. **What regulatory requirements exist for spectrum allocation, tower siting, and infrastructure sharing?**
   - TCSI spectrum allocation: What frequencies are available, what's the auction/allocation process, what do licenses cost?
   - Tower siting: Land ownership issues, environmental approvals, community consultation requirements
   - Infrastructure sharing mandates: Does TCSI require tower/backhaul sharing? What terms?
   - Right-of-way access for fiber/backhaul deployment

9. **How does SATSOL's ISO 27001 (information security) and ISO 9001 (quality management) certification support reliable backhaul?**
   - What operational standards do these certifications demonstrate?
   - How do security and quality management certifications affect telecom partnership viability?
   - What SLA (service level agreement) terms are typical for backhaul partnerships?

## Key Considerations

### Geographic Reality
- 1,000+ islands across 1,500km of ocean, 85% rural population
- Many islands have <1,000 residents—economics of coverage are challenging
- Urban concentration in Honiara creates hybrid deployment strategy need

### SATSOL Partnership Advantages
- **Starlink exclusivity:** Only authorized reseller in Solomon Islands
- **Existing infrastructure:** Fiber network, microwave links, data center facilities
- **Enterprise relationships:** Government, NGOs, businesses already use SATSOL for connectivity
- **ISO certifications:** Proven operational standards for reliability and security

### Infrastructure Constraints
- Limited electricity access (14-16% of population)—sites need solar + battery
- Cyclone/earthquake risk requires hardened infrastructure
- Land ownership disputes can delay tower deployment
- Supply chain challenges for equipment import and maintenance
