## Notes on Source Quality

**Highest Confidence (Tier 1):**
- Primary regulatory documents (Telecommunications Act 2009, TCSI directives)
- Official government publications (gazettes, policy documents)
- ITU technical presentations with verified data
- International development organization reports (World Bank, ADB, UNDP)

**High Confidence (Tier 2):**
- Established research institutions (Lowy Institute, ANU, USP)
- Industry associations with verification processes (GSMA, PITA)
- Regional regulatory bodies (Vanuatu TRBR)
- Technical standard organizations

**Medium Confidence (Tier 3):**
- News media (verify specific claims with primary sources)
- Company websites (note potential marketing bias)
- Industry reports (check methodology and funding sources)
- User-generated content (Wikipedia, wikis - verify all facts)

**Known Gaps:**
- Recent independent market share surveys (latest figures are estimates)
- Operator financial statements (not publicly available, especially Bmobile)
- Commercial terms for interconnection agreements (confidential)
- SINBIP tower-sharing pricing (not yet disclosed)
- Current TCSI policy positions on third operator licensing

**Source Validation Approach:**
- Facts confirmed by 3+ independent sources treated as verified
- Single-source claims flagged and attributed
- Contradictions reconciled or noted for further investigation
- Date of information tracked (some data from 2014-2018 period)
