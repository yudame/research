# Research Prompt: Episode 5 - Launch Execution

## Episode Context

**Series:** Solomon Islands Telecom Launch Series (Episode 5 of 6)

**Episode Title:** Launch Execution - From Spectrum to SIM Cards

**Previous Episode Context:**
- Episode 1: Financial ecosystem—75% unbanked, massive mobile money opportunity
- Episode 2: Duopoly market structure, regulatory landscape, strategic positioning options
- Episode 3: SATSOL partnership enables capital-efficient infrastructure deployment (fiber + Starlink)
- Episode 4: IumiCash partnership provides mobile money capabilities from day one

**This Episode Focus:** The operational reality of launching a third mobile network operator—regulatory approvals, network rollout sequencing, distribution channel buildout, pricing strategy, customer acquisition, and go-to-market execution. How strategy becomes operational reality.

**Target Audience:** Telecommunications operators, market entry specialists, business development executives, and investors interested in practical execution of telecom market entry.

---

## Research Objectives

Understand the end-to-end operational requirements for launching a mobile network operator in Solomon Islands. Map the regulatory approval process, timeline from application to commercial launch, network coverage prioritization strategy, distribution channel buildout, pricing and promotional tactics, customer acquisition approaches, and customer support infrastructure requirements.

---

## Core Research Questions

### Regulatory Approvals and Licensing

1. **What licenses, spectrum allocations, and regulatory approvals are required from TCSI to operate as third MNO?**
   - What specific licenses are required (telecommunications license, mobile network operator license, spectrum license)?
   - What is the application process, required documentation, capital/financial requirements?
   - What spectrum frequencies are available for new entrants, and what is the allocation mechanism (auction, beauty contest, first-come-first-served)?
   - What spectrum costs exist (license fees, annual spectrum fees)?
   - What interconnection agreements must be negotiated with Our Telekom and Bmobile?
   - What consumer protection obligations exist (service quality standards, complaint resolution, billing transparency)?
   - What other approvals are needed (tower siting, environmental, import permits for equipment)?

2. **What is the typical timeline from regulatory approval to commercial launch in Pacific markets?**
   - How long does TCSI licensing typically take (months/years)?
   - What parallel workstreams can proceed before full licensing (infrastructure deployment, team building, supplier contracts)?
   - What examples exist from recent Pacific market entries (Digicel launches, other new entrants)?
   - What delays or obstacles commonly arise (spectrum contention, interconnection disputes, regulatory reviews)?

### Network Rollout Strategy

3. **How do you design initial network coverage strategy—which islands/regions first, and why?**
   - **Population prioritization:** Honiara first (urban concentration), then provincial capitals, then rural islands?
   - **Economic prioritization:** Target highest GDP/commercial activity areas?
   - **Strategic prioritization:** Underserved areas where incumbents have weak coverage?
   - **Political considerations:** Government priorities, SINBIP tower locations, national development goals?
   - What percentage population coverage is target for launch day vs. 6 months vs. 12 months?
   - How do you sequence coverage expansion across 1,000+ islands realistically?

4. **What is the minimum viable network for commercial launch?**
   - How many tower sites are needed for launch (urban core only vs. multi-island coverage)?
   - What core network equipment is required (mobile switching, packet core, IMS, billing systems)?
   - What backhaul capacity is needed at launch (Starlink terminals, fiber connections)?
   - What operational support systems must be ready (billing, CRM, customer care, network monitoring)?
   - What testing and certification is required before commercial launch?

### Distribution and Sales

5. **How do you build distribution channels for SIM cards across dispersed islands?**
   - **Retail locations:** Operator-owned shops in Honiara and provincial centers?
   - **Agent networks:** Leveraging IumiCash agents, recruiting independent retailers, mobile agents?
   - **Partnerships:** SATSOL's existing retail presence, other strategic partnerships?
   - **Online sales:** E-commerce for SIM delivery to outer islands?
   - What inventory management, commission structures, and training are required for distribution partners?
   - How many distribution points are needed at launch vs. 6 months post-launch?

6. **How do you leverage SATSOL's existing business/enterprise relationships for B2B customer acquisition?**
   - What enterprise customers does SATSOL serve (government, NGOs, businesses)?
   - What B2B value propositions work (dedicated connectivity, bundled services, corporate mobile plans)?
   - Can existing SATSOL customer relationships provide early revenue and reference customers?
   - What enterprise sales cycles and contract structures are typical?

7. **How do you leverage IumiCash's merchant network as distribution channel for SIM cards?**
   - How many IumiCash merchants/agents exist, and where are they located?
   - What incentives motivate merchants to sell SIM cards (commissions, cross-promotion, bundled value)?
   - Can IumiCash agents handle SIM activation and customer onboarding?
   - What training and support do agents need to sell telecom services (not just mobile money)?

### Pricing and Positioning

8. **What pricing strategy breaks through the duopoly?**
   - **Price competition:** Undercut incumbents on data, voice, SMS pricing?
   - **Promotional pricing:** Launch offers, first X months free, referral bonuses?
   - **Bundled pricing:** Connectivity + mobile money packages, family plans, data-focused plans?
   - **Segment pricing:** Different offers for urban youth, rural users, businesses, remittance receivers?
   - What price elasticity exists (how price-sensitive are Solomon Islands mobile customers)?
   - What pricing transparency regulations apply (TCSI consumer protection rules)?

9. **How do you communicate brand positioning and service differentiation to drive trial?**
   - What brand messaging resonates (challenger brand, local empowerment, financial inclusion, innovation)?
   - What media channels work (radio, social media, outdoor advertising, community events)?
   - What language considerations exist (Pijin vs. English messaging)?
   - What role does word-of-mouth and community trust play in adoption?
   - How do you differentiate against incumbents (integrated mobile money, better coverage, superior service)?

### Customer Acquisition

10. **How do you acquire early adopters in a market without number portability?**
   - **Dual SIM strategy:** Target customers willing to carry second SIM for data or specific services?
   - **New connections:** First-time mobile users, young adults getting first phone?
   - **Complete switchers:** What motivates customers to abandon existing number and fully switch?
   - **Business customers:** Less number-dependent, willing to switch for better service/pricing?
   - What customer acquisition costs (CAC) are realistic, and what payback periods?

11. **What customer segments should you target first?**
   - **Urban youth:** Smartphone users, data-hungry, mobile money savvy?
   - **Rural unconnected:** Areas with weak incumbent coverage, first-time connectivity?
   - **Businesses:** Enterprise customers via SATSOL relationships?
   - **Remittance recipients:** Diaspora connections, IumiCash MTS license advantage?
   - **Price-sensitive users:** Those frustrated with incumbent pricing?
   - What sequencing makes sense (urban first then rural, or underserved areas first)?

12. **What handset strategy balances 4G/LTE capability requirements with customer affordability?**
   - What percentage of population owns smartphones vs. feature phones currently?
   - What handset subsidy or financing programs make sense?
   - Should you sell devices directly, or partner with retailers/importers?
   - What devices are already popular in market (brands, price points)?
   - How does device strategy relate to mobile money adoption (IumiCash requires smartphones)?

### Customer Support

13. **What customer support infrastructure is required across islands?**
   - **Call centers:** In-house vs. outsourced, local vs. regional, capacity planning?
   - **Retail support:** Walk-in customer service at operator shops?
   - **Agent support:** Can distribution agents handle basic customer service?
   - **Digital support:** App-based chat, email, social media support?
   - **Language support:** Pijin, English, other local languages?
   - What support volumes should you plan for (calls/tickets per 1,000 subscribers)?
   - What SLA targets for response time, resolution time, customer satisfaction?

### Operational Readiness

14. **What operational systems must be ready for launch?**
   - **Billing system:** Prepaid vs. postpaid, real-time charging, payment processing
   - **CRM system:** Customer data, service history, support ticketing
   - **Network monitoring:** Performance management, fault detection, capacity planning
   - **Fraud management:** SIM boxing prevention, revenue assurance, AML compliance
   - **Partner integration:** SATSOL backhaul management, IumiCash payment integration, interconnection with Our Telekom/Bmobile
   - What staff hiring and training is required (network operations, customer support, sales, finance)?

## Key Considerations

### No Number Portability Challenge
- Customers can't bring existing numbers from Our Telekom or Bmobile
- Acquisition limited to new connections, dual SIM users, or complete switchers
- Requires stronger value proposition to overcome switching friction

### Partnership Integration
- SATSOL provides enterprise customer access and retail presence
- IumiCash provides agent network and integrated service value proposition
- Need coordinated go-to-market strategy across partners

### Geographic Complexity
- 1,000+ islands, 85% rural, dispersed population
- Distribution and support infrastructure must reach beyond urban centers
- Sequenced rollout strategy essential (can't cover everything at launch)

### Market Maturity
- Incumbents have established distribution, brand awareness, customer loyalty
- Market entry requires both investment and patience for customer acquisition
- Profitability timeline likely measured in years, not months
