## Key Source Notes

**Cross-validation methodology:**
- Perplexity provided 86 academic and official sources with emphasis on peer-reviewed studies and meta-analyses
- Grok provided real-time market data, recent news, and practitioner perspectives from Solomon Islands and Pacific region
- Gemini provided comprehensive regulatory and policy framework analysis with 48+ government and international organization sources

**Conflicting data resolved:**
- Smartphone penetration: Converged on 42-42.5% (2025) across DataReportal and GSMA Intelligence
- Mobile wallet accounts: 13,400 total with 10-15% active (Pacific E-commerce Portal March 2024 assessment)
- 4G coverage: 25% as of 2020 (multiple sources); newer data pending infrastructure rollout

**Source quality assessment:**
- Tier 1 sources represent peer-reviewed, government official, or international organization authoritative data
- Tier 2 sources represent credible industry research, development organization reports, and case studies
- Tier 3 sources represent news, local market observations, and practitioner perspectives - valuable for context but requires cross-validation

**Notable evidence gaps acknowledged in research-briefing.md:**
- No peer-reviewed RCTs on Pacific island smartphone adoption specifically
- Limited long-term retention data for device financing in subsistence economies
- No formal evaluations of digital literacy program effectiveness in Pacific context
- Cultural factors beyond gender largely unstudied
