# Episode 9: Sustainability and Equity

## Research Prompt

Research the long-term sustainability challenges and equity considerations for micro-school models.

**Context:** Many alternative education models fail or become exclusive enclaves. This episode examines how to build sustainable, accessible micro-schools.

**Research questions:**
- What are the most common reasons micro-schools fail in the first 3 years?
- How do you prevent educator burnout in small, intensive learning environments?
- What are realistic cost models for micro-schools at different price points?
- How do successful micro-schools maintain quality while keeping costs accessible?
- What equity concerns arise with alternative education (exclusion, self-selection, disabilities)?
- How do you serve diverse learners, including those with learning differences?
- What role do school choice programs, scholarships, or sliding scales play?
- How do you build community and avoid isolation for both children and educators?

**Note:** Address both the business sustainability and the social equity dimensions.
