# Episode 8: Autonomy and Choice

## Research Prompt

Research the balance between child autonomy and adult guidance in hobby selection, extracurricular activities, and learning paths.

**Context:** A core tension in alternative education is how much choice to give children. This episode examines the research on autonomy, intrinsic motivation, and the role of adult guidance.

**Research questions:**
- What does research say about children choosing their own activities vs. parent-directed choices?
- What is the "sampling vs. specializing" debate, and what does evidence suggest?
- How do you support intrinsic motivation while still ensuring exposure to important skills?
- When does adult guidance help vs. undermine a child's development?
- How do you handle a child who wants to quit everything or who won't try new things?
- What role does boredom play in creativity and self-direction?
- How do successful alternative programs balance structure with student choice?

**Note:** Focus on practical guidance for parents and educators navigating these decisions.
