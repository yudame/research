# Research Brief: Building a Micro School: Ep. 2, The 2-Hour Core Model

**Date:** 2026-01-05
**Episode:** Building a Micro School: Ep. 2, The 2-Hour Core Model

---

## Research Topic

Research the evidence and implementation of compressed core academics models like Alpha School's 2-Hour Learning approach

## Key Questions

- What evidence exists for compressed academic learning models? What outcomes are measured?
- How do AI tutoring platforms (Alpha School's 2-Hour Learning, Khan Academy, others) actually work?
- What does the research say about AI-assisted learning for elementary-age children?
- What are the criticisms and concerns (Pennsylvania's charter denial, lack of accountability data)?
- How do "guides" differ from teachers in these models? What training do they need?
- What happens during the non-academic hours? How is that time structured?
- Is this model replicable at lower cost, or is it dependent on specific proprietary platforms?

## Context

Alpha School claims students complete core academics in 2 hours using AI-driven adaptive software, progressing at 2.6x the pace of traditional schools. This episode critically examines the model, maintaining a critical perspective to examine both promise and concerns including Pennsylvania's charter denial and lack of accountability data.
