### Phase 2: Question Discovery

**Created:** 2026-01-05

**After analyzing Perplexity's academic research, here are the questions we should investigate:**

#### What subtopics and themes emerged?

Major subtopics with extensive coverage:
- AI tutoring systems (ITS) effectiveness in K-12 (meta-analyses, effect sizes 0.68)
- Mastery learning frameworks (effect size 0.59, medium-large)
- Compressed schedules (four-day weeks, mixed results)
- Cognitive load theory and learning efficiency
- Competency-based education (Lindsay Unified case study)
- Student burnout and well-being (70% adolescents report academic burnout)
- Digital equity and infrastructure barriers (33% lack access)
- Teacher professional development requirements

Brief mentions:
- Alpha School's specific implementation (reported top 2% performance, lacks peer review)
- Bloom's 2 sigma problem and tutoring effects
- Spaced practice vs. massed practice

#### What gaps exist in the academic literature?

- **No peer-reviewed RCTs on Alpha School or 2-Hour Learning specifically**
- Elementary-specific research is sparse (most studies are secondary/higher ed)
- Long-term retention data missing (most studies measure immediate post-intervention only)
- Cost-benefit analysis of implementing compressed + AI models
- Replicability outside well-resourced private schools
- "Guide" role definition and training requirements (not found in research)
- Non-academic time structure and effectiveness (what happens during the other 6 hours?)

#### What recent developments aren't covered?

- **Pennsylvania charter denial of Alpha School** (mentioned in research-prompt.md but not in academic research)
- Current 2024-2025 Alpha School enrollment and outcomes
- Other micro-schools using similar models (are there competitors/alternatives?)
- Recent AI advances (GPT-4, Claude, etc.) in education (most research pre-2023)
- Regulatory/accountability frameworks for micro-schools
- Parent perspectives and satisfaction data

#### What contradictions or uncertainties need more sources?

- **Alpha School claims (top 2%, 2x learning speed) vs. absence of peer-reviewed validation**
- Four-day weeks show negative effects, but 2-Hour Learning claims positive effects (what's different?)
- ITS shows medium effects (0.68) but Alpha claims extraordinary performance (statistical outlier?)
- Scaling effects: tutoring effectiveness drops dramatically at scale (42% reduction) - how does this affect AI tutoring claims?
- Cognitive offloading concerns (Penn study shows AI harms learning) vs. Harvard study (AI tutoring doubles learning gains) - what explains the difference?

#### What industry/implementation questions arose?

- **What AI platforms does Alpha School actually use?** (Not specified in research)
- Cost per student for Alpha School model vs. traditional schools
- Teacher-to-student ratios in the "guide" model
- How are "guides" recruited, trained, and compensated?
- What happens when the AI system fails or students get stuck?
- How do families access Alpha School (admissions, tuition, location constraints)?
- Business model sustainability - is this scalable or niche?

#### What policy/regulatory angles need investigation?

- **Pennsylvania charter denial specifics** - what were the exact concerns?
- Accountability frameworks for micro-schools (how are they evaluated?)
- Accreditation and diploma recognition
- Teacher certification requirements for "guides"
- Data privacy and student information protection in AI systems
- Compliance with IDEA and special education requirements

#### What practitioner perspectives are missing?

- What are educators on X/Twitter saying about compressed learning models?
- Parent testimonials beyond Alpha School marketing materials
- Students who left Alpha School - what were their experiences?
- Traditional teachers' critiques of the model
- Education researchers' skepticism or concerns (beyond peer-reviewed papers)
