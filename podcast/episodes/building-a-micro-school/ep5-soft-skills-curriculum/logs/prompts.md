### Phase 2: Question Discovery & Gap Analysis

**Analysis completed:** 2026-01-22

After analyzing Perplexity's comprehensive academic research (7,958 words, 33+ studies), here are the questions we should investigate:

#### What subtopics and themes emerged?

**Well-covered areas:**
- Five CASEL competencies (self-awareness, self-management, social awareness, relationship skills, responsible decision-making)
- Meta-analyses of SEL programs (PATHS, Second Step, Positive Action)
- Academic-SEL integration and bidirectional relationships
- Assessment methods (portfolios, observation, student reflection)
- Teacher professional development requirements
- Mixed-age classroom benefits
- Project-based learning for SEL development

**Briefly mentioned areas:**
- Culturally responsive SEL practices
- Implementation challenges (time, funding, training)
- Specific meditation/mindfulness techniques
- Technology integration with SEL

#### What gaps exist in the academic literature?

1. **Micro-school specific implementation** - All studies focus on traditional classroom settings (20-30 students), not small cohorts (8-12 students)
2. **Cost-benefit analysis for small settings** - Economic feasibility of SEL curricula in micro-schools
3. **Parent involvement in SEL** - How do families integrate with school-based SEL in intimate settings?
4. **Assessment in ultra-small groups** - How do assessment methods change with 8-12 students vs. 25-30?
5. **Hybrid/multi-age implementation** - Limited research on SEL in intentionally multi-age micro-schools
6. **Comparison of SEL curricula** - Which programs work best in which contexts?

#### What recent developments aren't covered?

1. **Post-2020 SEL evolution** - How has SEL changed after pandemic-era social isolation?
2. **AI tools for SEL** - Emerging tech for emotion recognition, feedback, assessment
3. **New micro-school models** - Recent examples of schools successfully implementing intentional SEL
4. **Current policy debates** - State-level SEL mandates, controversies, opt-out movements
5. **Recent program launches** - New SEL curricula designed for 2024-2026 launch

#### What contradictions or uncertainties need more sources?

1. **Frequency vs. quality trade-offs** - PATHS effective at once/week, but other research emphasizes daily practice
2. **Explicit vs. integrated SEL** - When is standalone SEL time better than integrated instruction?
3. **Assessment reliability** - Limited validation research on non-standardized SEL assessment methods
4. **Effect size variation** - Why do some studies show 0.05 effects while others show 0.50+?
5. **Long-term persistence** - Do SEL gains fade out, or do they compound over time?

#### What industry/implementation questions arose?

1. **What do successful micro-schools actually do for SEL?** - Case studies of 8-15 student schools
2. **How much does quality SEL cost?** - Budget requirements for curricula, training, materials in small settings
3. **What does "high-fidelity implementation" look like?** - Observable teacher behaviors, lesson structures
4. **How do schools assess without standardized tests?** - Practical portfolio systems, observation protocols
5. **What SEL tools work in mixed-age settings?** - Adapting curricula for 4-9 year-olds simultaneously

#### What policy/regulatory angles need investigation?

1. **State SEL standards** - Which states mandate SEL? What are requirements?
2. **Micro-school regulations** - Do small private schools face different SEL requirements?
3. **Accreditation expectations** - What SEL documentation do accreditors require?
4. **Parent rights debates** - Current controversies around opt-out, transparency, content
5. **Evidence-based program lists** - Which SEL programs meet ESSA Tier 1/2 standards?

#### What practitioner perspectives are missing?

1. **Micro-school founder experiences** - What SEL approaches actually work in small settings?
2. **Parent testimonials** - How do families experience intentional SEL in micro-schools?
3. **Teacher challenges** - What's hardest about implementing SEL in practice?
4. **Student voice** - How do elementary students experience explicit SEL instruction?
5. **Failed implementations** - What didn't work and why?
