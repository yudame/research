# Research Brief: Building a Micro School: Ep. 5, The Soft Skills Curriculum

**Date:** 2026-01-22
**Episode:** Building a Micro School: Ep. 5, The Soft Skills Curriculum

---

## Research Topic

Research how to deliberately develop social-emotional and interpersonal skills in elementary-age children (ages 4-9). Focus on which soft skills predict success, explicit teaching methods, SEL curricula for small settings, assessment approaches, and the role of project-based learning and mixed-age interactions.

## Key Questions

- What soft skills are most predictive of long-term success, and at what ages are they best developed?
- How do you teach empathy, resilience, communication, and collaboration explicitly?
- What curricula or frameworks exist for social-emotional learning (SEL) in small settings?
- How do you assess soft skills development without standardized testing?
- What's the relationship between academic instruction and soft skills—are they competing or complementary?
- How do project-based learning and real-world challenges develop soft skills?
- What role do mixed-age interactions play in soft skills development?

## Context

Traditional curricula often over-index on academics while soft skills develop incidentally. Micro-schools have the flexibility to design intentional soft skills development for elementary-age children (ages 4-9). This research will focus on practical curriculum design and assessment approaches.
