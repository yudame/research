# Episode 6: Mentors and Apprenticeships

## Research Prompt

Research the role of non-parental adult mentors and early career exposure in child development.

**Context:** Micro-schools can leverage community connections to provide mentorship and real-world learning experiences that traditional schools cannot easily replicate.

**Research questions:**
- What does research say about the developmental impact of non-parental adult mentors?
- At what ages can children benefit from job shadowing or apprenticeship-style experiences?
- How do you structure meaningful mentor relationships for young children?
- What are successful models of community involvement in small learning environments?
- How do you vet and train community mentors for child safety?
- What types of real-world projects or experiences are developmentally appropriate for ages 4-9?
- How do you balance exposure to adult work worlds with protecting childhood?

**Note:** Focus on practical implementation of mentor programs and community partnerships.
