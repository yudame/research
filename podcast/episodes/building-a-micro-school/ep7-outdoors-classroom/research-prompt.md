# Episode 7: Outdoors as Classroom

## Research Prompt

Research the practical implementation of outdoor and nature-based education in micro-school settings.

**Context:** Forest schools and outdoor education show strong benefits, but practical implementation requires addressing weather, safety, curriculum integration, and logistics.

**Research questions:**
- What does research show about cognitive, physical, and social-emotional benefits of outdoor learning?
- How do forest schools and nature-based programs structure their time and curriculum?
- What's the minimum "dose" of outdoor time that produces measurable benefits?
- How do you handle weather, safety, and liability concerns for outdoor education?
- What outdoor activities are developmentally appropriate for different ages (4-5, 6-7, 8-9)?
- How do you integrate academic content with outdoor experiences?
- What equipment, spaces, and resources are needed for effective outdoor programming?

**Note:** Focus on practical implementation—what does a week actually look like?
