## Phase 1: Perplexity Academic Research

**Created:** 2026-01-02
**Tool:** Perplexity API (sonar-deep-research)
**Focus:** Academic & Official Sources
**Expected Duration:** 30-120 seconds

**Prompt:**

```
Research the practical operational aspects of running a micro-school kindergarten for ages 4-5, including physical space design, daily scheduling, staffing ratios, regulatory compliance, and financial sustainability.

**Research methodology:**
- Prioritize peer-reviewed studies, meta-analyses, systematic reviews, and authoritative sources
- Distinguish between correlation and causation in findings
- Report effect sizes and practical significance, not just statistical significance
- Note the study populations and whether findings generalize to relevant demographics
- Compare individual studies against meta-analyses and systematic reviews
- Identify preliminary research vs. well-replicated findings
- Note funding sources and potential conflicts of interest when relevant
- Include contradictory findings and areas of scientific uncertainty
- Cite specific studies, researchers, and sources throughout
- Provide full source URLs for all citations

**Output:** Comprehensive research report with extensive citations, sample sizes, methodological details, and source links.
```

## Phase 2: Question Discovery

**After analyzing Perplexity's academic research, here are the questions we should investigate:**

### What subtopics and themes emerged?
- Physical space design (square footage requirements, learning center organization, safety/accessibility)
- Daily scheduling and curriculum (play-based vs. structured instruction, time allocation)
- Staffing ratios and teacher qualifications (evidence on optimal ratios, credential requirements)
- Regulatory compliance (licensing, health/safety, teacher certification)
- Financial sustainability (startup costs, operating expenses, tuition pricing)
- **Strong coverage**: Academic research on developmental outcomes, longitudinal studies
- **Brief mentions**: Actual practitioner experiences, recent micro-school movement trends

### What gaps exist in the academic literature?
- Perplexity focused heavily on traditional preschool/kindergarten research, not micro-school specific models
- Limited coverage of "micro-school" as a distinct movement (mentions traditional ECE research)
- No data on micro-school-specific startup experiences or case studies
- Missing: Real-world implementation stories from actual micro-school founders
- Missing: Regional variations in regulatory enforcement (what rules are actually applied vs. on paper)
- Missing: Specific examples of daily schedules from actual operating micro-schools

### What recent developments aren't covered?
- Perplexity covers research through ~2024 but may miss very recent micro-school movement trends
- Post-COVID growth in micro-schools and learning pods (2020-2025)
- Recent state legislation changes affecting small private schools
- Current practitioner discussions about what's working/failing in 2025-2026
- Tech tools and platforms supporting micro-school operations (recent developments)

### What contradictions or uncertainties need more sources?
- Class size effects: Perplexity notes mixed evidence and methodological critiques of STAR study
- Play-based vs. structured instruction: Evidence supports both, but optimal balance unclear
- Ratio importance: Meta-analysis found "limited evidence" but NAEYC recommends 1:10 - need practitioner perspectives on this tension
- Teacher qualifications vs. ratios: Which matters more when resources are constrained?

### What industry/implementation questions arose?
- What do actual startup costs look like for micro-schools launched in 2020-2025? (Perplexity gives general ECE estimates)
- What business models work? (tuition-only, hybrid public funding, cooperative models)
- How are successful micro-schools handling licensing in different states?
- What curriculum platforms or resources are micro-schools actually using?
- What technology infrastructure is needed (student management systems, communication tools)?
- How do micro-schools market and recruit families?

### What policy/regulatory angles need investigation?
- State-by-state comparison of micro-school regulations (Perplexity gives general overview)
- How are different states defining "micro-school" vs. "private school" vs. "childcare"?
- Recent legislative changes (2023-2025) affecting small private schools
- ESA (Education Savings Account) programs and micro-school eligibility by state
- How strict is enforcement? (What's required on paper vs. what inspectors actually check)

### What practitioner perspectives are missing?
- What do micro-school founders say are the biggest challenges in Year 1?
- What mistakes do beginners make that could be avoided?
- What's the real-world time commitment for a founder-teacher?
- How do micro-school families find each other and build community?
- What does a typical week look like for a micro-school kindergarten teacher?
- What are parents actually looking for when choosing a micro-school?
