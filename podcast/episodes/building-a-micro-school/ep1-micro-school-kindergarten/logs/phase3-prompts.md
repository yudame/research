**Based on:** Phase 2 question discovery analysis of Perplexity results

## GPT-Researcher Prompt (Industry & Case Studies)

**Focus:** Micro-school startup experiences, business models, implementation case studies

```
Research micro-school kindergarten startups and operations, focusing on these specific questions:

**Industry Analysis:**
- What are actual startup costs for micro-schools launched between 2020-2025?
- What business models are working? (tuition-only, ESA-funded, cooperative models, hybrid approaches)
- What curriculum platforms and resources are micro-schools actually using for kindergarten?

**Case Studies & Implementation:**
- What specific examples exist of successful micro-school kindergartens serving ages 4-5?
- What technology infrastructure do micro-schools need? (student management systems, parent communication tools)
- How do micro-schools market to families and build enrollment?

**Comparative Analysis:**
- How do micro-school operational costs compare to traditional private kindergarten programs?
- What lessons can be learned from the learning pod movement (2020-2022) for micro-school sustainability?

Focus on: Case studies of actual micro-schools, operational data, business analysis, technology platforms, market research on family demand.
Provide comprehensive findings with citations, specific examples, and comparative analysis where relevant.
```

## Gemini Deep Research Prompt (Policy & Regulatory Frameworks)

**Focus:** State regulations, ESA programs, licensing enforcement

```
Research micro-school kindergarten regulatory frameworks and education policy, focusing on these specific questions:

**Regulatory & Policy Frameworks:**
- How do different US states define and regulate "micro-schools" vs. "private schools" vs. "childcare centers"?
- What are state-by-state licensing requirements for small private kindergarten programs (5-15 students)?
- What recent legislative changes (2023-2025) affect micro-schools and small private schools?

**Comparative Policy Analysis:**
- Which states have Education Savings Account (ESA) or school choice programs, and are micro-schools eligible?
- How strict is regulatory enforcement in practice? (What do inspectors actually check vs. what's written in regulations?)
- What states are most vs. least friendly to micro-school startups based on regulatory burden?

**Strategic Context:**
- What policy trends are emerging around micro-schools and educational choice?
- What lobbying efforts or advocacy organizations are shaping micro-school policy?

Focus on: State regulations, legislation, ESA program details, licensing enforcement practices, comparative policy analysis across states.
Provide findings with official source citations, effective dates, and regulatory context.
```

## Claude Research Prompt (Comprehensive Synthesis)

**Focus:** Multi-dimensional analysis connecting academic research to practical implementation

```
Research micro-school kindergarten operations for ages 4-5, focusing on these specific questions:

- How do you reconcile academic research (which focuses on traditional schools) with the micro-school model's unique constraints and advantages?
- What's the relationship between teacher qualifications and program sustainability in very small settings where founder-teachers wear multiple hats?
- How can micro-schools balance evidence-based practice (low ratios, qualified teachers, quality materials) with financial sustainability when serving only 5-15 students?

**Research methodology:**
- Conduct comprehensive research across academic, industry, policy, and practitioner sources
- Prioritize authoritative sources and distinguish correlation from causation
- Note methodological limitations and conflicts of interest
- Include contradictory findings and areas of uncertainty
- Cite specific studies, reports, and sources extensively with URLs
```

## Grok Prompt (X/Twitter Discourse & Real-Time Developments)

**Focus:** Current micro-school practitioner discussions, recent trends, what families are saying

```
Search X/Twitter and recent news for micro-school kindergarten operations and the micro-school movement.

**Active X/Twitter Debates (last 60 days):**
- Who are the active voices in the micro-school movement? (Founders, advocates, platforms - names, handles, credentials)
- What are they discussing? (Challenges, successes, policy changes, practical tips)
- What's the sentiment? (Are people optimistic about micro-schools, or highlighting challenges?)

**Practitioner Insights & Frustrations:**
- What are micro-school founders saying about their biggest Year 1 challenges?
- What do they say academic research "misses" about running a micro-school?
- What practical tips are being shared? (What curriculum to use, how to handle licensing, family recruitment)

**Recent Developments (last 6 months):**
- Any new micro-school platforms, networks, or support organizations launched?
- State legislation changes affecting micro-schools or ESA programs?
- Notable success stories or failures being discussed?

**Family Perspectives:**
- What are parents saying they want from micro-schools?
- What concerns or questions are prospective micro-school families asking?

**Output format:**
- Name every source (person + handle + credential/affiliation + date)
- Tag credibility: [HIGH] = micro-school founder/operator, [MED] = involved parent/educator, [LOW] = general observer
- Include X post URLs where possible
- Distinguish opinion from news reporting
```
