# Episode 1: The Micro-School Kindergarten

## Research Prompt

Research the practical operational aspects of running a micro-school kindergarten for ages 4-5.

**Context:** This episode focuses on implementation rather than developmental science (covered in our Kindergarten First Principles series). The audience is parents or educators considering starting a small learning environment.

**Research questions:**
- What does the physical space design look like for a small kindergarten (5-15 children)?
- How do successful micro-school kindergartens structure their daily rhythm?
- What are the practical challenges of maintaining recommended ratios (7-10:1)?
- How do you balance structured learning time with play-based approaches in practice?
- What licensing, safety, and regulatory considerations apply to small learning environments?
- What do startup costs and ongoing operational budgets look like?
- What are common first-year mistakes and how to avoid them?

**Note:** Reference developmental science from Kindergarten First Principles series; focus here on practical "how to" rather than "why."
