# Research Brief: Building a Micro School: Ep. 1, The Micro-School Kindergarten

**Date:** 2026-01-02
**Episode:** Building a Micro School: Ep. 1, The Micro-School Kindergarten

---

## Research Topic

Research the practical operational aspects of running a micro-school kindergarten for ages 4-5. Focus on physical space design, daily rhythm structure, ratio implementation, licensing considerations, startup costs, and common first-year mistakes.

## Key Questions

- What does the physical space design look like for a small kindergarten (5-15 children)?
- How do successful micro-school kindergartens structure their daily rhythm?
- What are the practical challenges of maintaining recommended ratios (7-10:1)?
- How do you balance structured learning time with play-based approaches in practice?
- What licensing, safety, and regulatory considerations apply to small learning environments?
- What do startup costs and ongoing operational budgets look like?
- What are common first-year mistakes and how to avoid them?

## Context

This episode focuses on implementation rather than developmental science (covered in the Kindergarten First Principles series). The audience is parents or educators considering starting a small learning environment. We're looking for practical "how to" guidance rather than "why" this matters developmentally.
