## Phase 2: Question Discovery

**After analyzing Perplexity's academic research, here are the questions we should investigate:**

### What subtopics and themes emerged?
- **Screen time effects**: Dose-dependent relationships, executive function impacts, ADHD connections
- **Passive vs. active screen time**: Video deficit effect, social contingency, co-viewing benefits
- **Educational technology effectiveness**: Math apps (d=0.31), coding/computational thinking (d=1.62), literacy interventions, blended learning
- **Special populations**: ASD interventions, ADHD considerations, motor skills development
- **Implementation factors**: Teacher training, content quality evaluation, blended learning ratios
- **Health/safety**: Blue light sensitivity, digital eye strain, sleep disruption
- **Equity issues**: Digital divide, socioeconomic disparities in access and outcomes

### What gaps exist in the academic literature?
- **Long-term outcomes**: Most studies are short-term (weeks to months); lacking multi-year longitudinal tracking
- **Dosage thresholds**: "2 hours/day" guideline not well-calibrated by age, content type, or context
- **Implementation quality**: Studies report "iPad use" without measuring fidelity, teacher skill, or pedagogical integration
- **Individual differences**: Limited research on temperament, prior experiences, home environment as moderators
- **Cultural context**: Most research from Western developed nations; limited generalizability

### What recent developments aren't covered?
- **2025-2026 edtech tools**: Academic research lags behind market by 2-3 years; what's actually being used now?
- **Post-pandemic implementation**: How have schools adapted technology use after COVID-19 remote learning?
- **AI-powered tutoring**: ChatGPT, Claude, and other LLM-based educational tools (too new for peer review)
- **Parent/educator sentiment shifts**: Has opinion changed post-pandemic about screen time balance?

### What contradictions or uncertainties need more sources?
- **Motor skills**: Conflicting findings on touchscreen impact (some show benefits, others show harm)
- **Optimal screen time duration**: Guidelines vary; "Goldilocks hypothesis" not well-tested
- **Content vs. context effects**: Hard to separate whether benefits come from what kids watch or how they watch it

### What industry/implementation questions arose?
- **Minimum viable tech stack**: What's the practical minimum for a micro-school (not "what's ideal")?
- **Device management**: How do successful programs handle charging, updates, breakage, sharing?
- **App evaluation workflow**: How do teachers/administrators actually vet apps in practice?
- **Cost-effectiveness**: ROI on different technology investments for small schools
- **Interoperability**: Do different tools/platforms work together, or create integration headaches?

### What policy/regulatory angles need investigation?
- **Screen time policies**: What do districts/states actually mandate vs. recommend?
- **Data privacy/COPPA**: Legal requirements for edtech in K-5 settings
- **Teacher certification**: Do states require edtech training for elementary teacher licensure?
- **Accessibility requirements**: ADA/504 compliance for digital learning materials
- **Funding streams**: IDEA, Title I, E-rate, state edtech grants - what's available?

### What practitioner perspectives are missing?
- **Micro-school operators**: What are actual founders/teachers saying works in small settings?
- **Parent concerns**: What are families actually worried about (not just what researchers study)?
- **Teacher frustrations**: What implementation barriers do educators face day-to-day?
- **Tech coordinator insights**: What do specialists say about managing edtech at small scale?
