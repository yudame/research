**Date:** 2026-01-21
**Episode:** Building a Micro School: Ep. 4, Technology as Infrastructure

## Research Topic

Research evidence-based approaches to technology integration in early childhood and elementary education for ages 4-9

## Key Questions

- What does current research say about screen time effects on children ages 4-9?
- Which educational technology tools have evidence of effectiveness for this age range?
- How do you implement technology without replacing human interaction?
- What are practical screen time policies that balance learning benefits with developmental concerns?
- How do you evaluate edtech claims critically? What metrics actually matter?
- What's the minimum viable technology stack for a micro-school?
- How do successful programs handle device management, content filtering, and digital citizenship?

## Context

Micro-schools must make practical decisions about which technologies to adopt, how much screen time is appropriate, and when technology helps vs. hinders learning. Focus on practical implementation decisions, not theoretical debates.
