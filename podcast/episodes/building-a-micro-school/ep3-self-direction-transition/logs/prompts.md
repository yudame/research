## Phase 2: Question Discovery

**After analyzing Perplexity's academic research, here are the questions we should investigate:**

### What subtopics and themes emerged?
- Self-directed learning (SDL) processes and 6-step frameworks
- Mastery-based learning systems (effect sizes: 0.5 SD improvement)
- Mixed-age groupings (6-9) with peer tutoring and looping
- Scaffolding frameworks (ZPD, contingency, fading, transfer)
- Metacognition development (monitoring, control, social metacognition)
- Productive struggle vs. unproductive frustration
- Differentiation strategies for heterogeneous ability ranges

### What gaps exist in the academic literature?
- **Implementation specifics**: Academic research describes theoretical frameworks but limited detail on day-to-day classroom management
- **Micro-school context**: Most research is in conventional schools; few studies examine 8-15 student environments
- **Age 6-9 specificity**: Research often groups K-5 together rather than focusing on this critical transition period
- **Scaffolding calibration**: How do teachers practically determine when to fade support for individual students?
- **Productive struggle thresholds**: Where is the line between productive and unproductive struggle for different children?

### What recent developments aren't covered?
- **Pandemic impact on self-direction**: How did 2020-2023 remote learning affect 6-9 year olds' capacity for independence?
- **AI tutoring tools**: How are adaptive learning platforms being used to support mastery progression?
- **Micro-school movement growth**: What has emerged in the last 12-18 months in small learning environments?
- **Parent-led learning pods**: What lessons from pandemic pods apply to ongoing micro-schools?

### What contradictions or uncertainties need more sources?
- **Ability grouping effects**: Meta-analyses show conflicting results (positive 0.2-0.3 SD vs. negative 0.2 SD for low-ability students)
- **Productive struggle evidence**: Qualitative studies support it, but quantitative evidence is limited and some studies favor direct instruction
- **Mixed-age benefits**: Some studies show benefits for younger students only; others show benefits for all ages
- **Teacher preparation requirements**: How much specialized training is truly needed for mixed-age classrooms?

### What industry/implementation questions arose?
- **Successful micro-school models**: What specific schools are implementing these principles effectively?
- **Curriculum materials**: What mastery-based curricula work well for 6-9 mixed-age groups?
- **Economic viability**: What are the costs of implementing these approaches in small settings?
- **Assessment systems**: What tools track mastery progression across ability ranges?
- **Parent communication**: How do successful programs communicate non-traditional progression to families?

### What policy/regulatory angles need investigation?
- **State requirements**: Do age-grade regulations create barriers for mixed-age groupings?
- **Standardized testing**: How do mastery-based schools handle grade-level testing requirements?
- **Accreditation**: What standards apply to non-traditional progression systems?
- **Teacher certification**: Are there special requirements for mixed-age teaching?

### What practitioner perspectives are missing?
- **Micro-school founders**: What do people actually running 6-9 programs say works/doesn't work?
- **Parent experiences**: How do families navigate the transition to self-directed learning?
- **Teacher challenges**: What daily struggles do teachers face that research doesn't capture?
- **Student voice**: How do children themselves experience this transition?
- **Regional variations**: Are there geographic differences in acceptance/implementation?
