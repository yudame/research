# Research Brief: Building a Micro School: Ep. 3, The Self-Direction Transition

**Date:** 2026-01-05
**Episode:** Building a Micro School: Ep. 3, The Self-Direction Transition

---

## Research Topic

Research how children ages 6-9 develop capacity for self-directed learning and how educational environments can support this transition.

## Key Questions

- What does research say about when children can effectively self-direct their learning?
- How do mastery-based progression systems work in practice for this age range?
- What are the benefits and challenges of mixed-age groupings (e.g., 6-9 together)?
- How do you scaffold the transition from guided to independent work?
- What role does metacognition play, and how do you develop it at these ages?
- What does "productive struggle" look like vs. frustration that shuts down learning?
- How do successful micro-schools handle the wide range of abilities in this age span?

## Context

This episode bridges kindergarten (highly guided) to independent learning, covering the developmental and practical aspects of increasing student autonomy for ages 6-9. Focus on practical implementation while grounding in developmental research.
