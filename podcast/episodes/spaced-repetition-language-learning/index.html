Episodes Coming Soon

Ep 1 The Science of Forgetting Coming soon
The neuroscience of memory formation, consolidation, and the forgetting curve.
More
Explore the biological mechanisms underlying the forgetting curve and memory decay at the neural level. Learn how memory consolidation works across different timescales (minutes to days), why spaced practice outperforms massed practice, how sleep affects retention, and what research reveals about optimal spacing intervals and the testing effect.
Ep 2 Algorithms and Scheduling Coming soon
How SRS systems translate neuroscience into practical review scheduling algorithms.
More
Discover how the SM-2 algorithm works and its evolution to modern approaches like FSRS and Half-Life Regression. Learn about the mathematical foundations of spaced repetition, how algorithms predict individual forgetting curves, trade-offs between simplicity and optimization, and how personalization adapts to individual learners.
Ep 3 The Mobile Learning Revolution Coming soon
How smartphones transformed SRS from niche technique to mainstream learning tool.
More
Examine how mobile access changed spaced repetition practice through bite-sized learning sessions. Explore notification algorithms and habit formation research, the impact of streak mechanics on motivation, how microlearning compares to longer study sessions, and the balance between accessibility and depth of learning in mobile UX design.
Ep 4 The App Ecosystem Coming soon
Comparative analysis of SRS apps and their different philosophies about language learning.
More
Compare the fundamental design philosophies of Anki, Duolingo, Memrise, Clozemaster, and LingQ. Examine trade-offs between user control versus guided experience, gamification versus customization, and different approaches to progression from beginner to advanced. Learn what research reveals about which approaches work for whom.
Ep 5 The Context Problem Coming soon
Critical examination of SRS limitations, the recognition-production gap, and transfer failure.
More
Investigate the recognition versus production gap in language learning and how flashcard performance relates to speaking ability. Explore research on context-dependent memory, decontextualized learning critiques, transfer failure to real communication, limitations of gamification, and the "illusion of competence" where learners excel at reviews but struggle in conversation.
Ep 6 Integration and Synthesis Coming soon
Building complete learning systems that combine SRS with comprehensible input and output practice.
More
Discover evidence-based practices for integrating spaced repetition with other learning modalities. Learn about context-rich flashcard design, supporting the transition from recognition to production, balancing efficiency with authenticity, how successful polyglots use SRS, emerging AI-powered personalization, and optimal time allocation across different activities for developing genuine communicative competence.
