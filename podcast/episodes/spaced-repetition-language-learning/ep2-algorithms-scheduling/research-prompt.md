# Episode 2: Algorithms and Scheduling

## Research Prompt

Research how spaced repetition systems (SRS) translate neuroscience insights into practical algorithms for scheduling reviews. Explore the evolution from early algorithms to modern machine learning approaches.

**Core questions to explore:**
- How does the SM-2 algorithm work? What are its core principles and mathematical foundations?
- What are the key differences between SM-2 and later algorithms (SM-15, SM-17, FSRS)?
- How does Half-Life Regression model individual forgetting curves? What data does it use to make predictions?
- What are the trade-offs between simple, transparent algorithms and complex, optimized ones?
- How do different algorithms handle the "ease factor" or difficulty rating of items?
- What does research show about optimal initial intervals and interval expansion rates?
- How do algorithmic predictions compare to actual student performance data?
- What role does personalization play? Can algorithms effectively adapt to individual learners?

**Research methodology:**
- Examine original papers describing SRS algorithms (Wozniak's SuperMemo research, Duolingo's published research)
- Look for empirical studies comparing algorithm effectiveness
- Distinguish between theoretical claims and validated performance data
- Note the datasets used for algorithm development and validation
- Identify limitations of current algorithmic approaches
- Consider the balance between algorithmic sophistication and user understanding
- Examine open-source implementations and their documented performance
