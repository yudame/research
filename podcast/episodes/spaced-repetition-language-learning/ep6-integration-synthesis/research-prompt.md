# Episode 6: Integration and Synthesis

## Research Prompt

Research how to build complete, effective language learning systems that integrate spaced repetition with other learning modalities. Explore best practices for combining SRS with comprehensible input, output practice, and authentic communication to develop genuine communicative competence.

**Core questions to explore:**
- How can spaced repetition be integrated with comprehensible input approaches? What does research show about optimal combinations?
- What are evidence-based practices for creating context-rich flashcards versus isolated word pairs?
- How can SRS support the transition from recognition to production? What role should output practice play?
- What does research show about balancing efficiency (SRS) with authenticity (real communication)?
- How do successful polyglots and language learners integrate SRS into broader learning systems?
- What is the evidence for sentence-based cards, cloze deletions, and other context-preserving formats?
- How can AI and language models enhance SRS by providing personalized, context-rich content?
- What are the emerging approaches combining SRS with conversation practice, reading, and listening?
- What does research suggest about the optimal role and time allocation for SRS in a complete learning program?
- How should SRS use change as learners progress from beginner to intermediate to advanced levels?

**Research methodology:**
- Look for studies on integrated learning approaches combining multiple methods
- Examine research on successful language learners' strategies and time allocation
- Investigate studies comparing different flashcard formats and their effectiveness
- Consider research on the progression from input to output in language acquisition
- Look for case studies of successful SRS integration in formal and informal learning
- Examine emerging research on AI-powered personalization and adaptive content
- Investigate studies on optimal practice distribution across different activities
- Consider research on deliberate practice and skill development in language learning
