# Episode 5: The Context Problem

## Research Prompt

Research the limitations and critiques of spaced repetition systems for language learning, particularly focusing on issues of decontextualized learning, the gap between recognition and production, transfer failure, and the potential downsides of gamification.

**Core questions to explore:**
- What is the recognition versus production gap in language learning? How does performance on flashcard reviews relate to actual speaking ability?
- What does research show about context-dependent memory? How does learning vocabulary in isolation affect the ability to use it in conversation?
- What is the evidence on transfer of learning from SRS practice to real communication contexts?
- What are the critiques of decontextualized flashcard learning from language acquisition theorists?
- How does spaced repetition relate to theories of comprehensible input (Krashen) and output practice (Swain)?
- What does research reveal about the limitations of gamification? How do extrinsic rewards affect intrinsic motivation over time?
- What are the documented cases of high SRS engagement but poor communicative competence?
- What does research show about the "illusion of competence" - feeling fluent in reviews but struggling in conversation?
- Are there learner profiles or contexts where SRS is particularly ineffective?

**Research methodology:**
- Examine critiques from second language acquisition researchers
- Look for studies measuring SRS learning transfer to communication tasks
- Investigate research on context-dependent memory and vocabulary knowledge
- Consider the distinction between declarative and procedural knowledge in language
- Examine longitudinal studies of app users' actual proficiency development
- Look for research on the gap between passive recognition and active production
- Investigate studies on gamification's long-term effects on motivation
- Note any research on "optimizing the metric" - high engagement without learning
