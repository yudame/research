# Episode 1: The Science of Forgetting

## Research Prompt

Research the neuroscience of memory formation, consolidation, and forgetting as it relates to learning new information, particularly language vocabulary and patterns.

**Core questions to explore:**
- What are the biological mechanisms underlying the forgetting curve? How does memory decay over time at the neural level?
- What happens during memory consolidation? How do different timescales (minutes, hours, days) involve different neurological processes?
- What is the evidence for spacing effects in learning? What experiments and meta-analyses demonstrate that spaced practice outperforms massed practice?
- How does sleep affect memory consolidation for newly learned material?
- What is the relationship between retrieval practice and memory strengthening? Why does testing enhance retention?
- How do optimal spacing intervals change based on the retention timeline? What does research show about the relationship between study gaps and test delays?

**Research methodology:**
- Prioritize peer-reviewed neuroscience and cognitive psychology research
- Look for meta-analyses and systematic reviews that synthesize multiple studies
- Distinguish between laboratory findings and real-world language learning contexts
- Report effect sizes and practical significance, not just statistical significance
- Note the populations studied (students, adults, specific age groups) and generalizability
- Identify contradictory findings and areas of ongoing debate
- Compare individual studies against larger meta-analyses
- Include both foundational research (Ebbinghaus forgetting curve) and recent neuroscience advances
