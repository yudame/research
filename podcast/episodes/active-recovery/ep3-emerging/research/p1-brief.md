## Research Topic

Emerging recovery technologies and advanced nutrition for a fit 40-year-old male, focusing on evidence-based assessment of next-generation recovery tools and nutritional interventions.

## Key Questions

- What is the evidence for electrical stimulation technologies (NMES, e-stim) in recovery?
- How effective are advanced recovery devices (infrared therapy, pneumatic compression, hyperbaric oxygen)?
- What evidence supports mind-body recovery techniques (mindfulness, breathing for cortisol/HRV)?
- Which recovery supplements have robust evidence (tart cherry juice, curcumin, bromelain)?
- What are the evidence-based nutrition planning principles (protein targets, carb timing, micronutrients)?
- How do we separate promising early research from robust evidence?
- What is the role of industry funding in recovery technology research?

## Context

This is Episode 3 of the Active Recovery series. Previous episodes covered foundational recovery principles (Ep. 1) and basic modalities like heat, cold, touch, and stretch (Ep. 2). This episode investigates cutting-edge recovery tools and nutritional interventions, with special emphasis on critical evaluation.

**Target audience:** 40-year-old male with established fitness routine seeking honest assessment of emerging recovery technologies and supplements.

**Critical approach:** Distinguish between promising early research and robust evidence. Note industry funding, small effect sizes, and methodological limitations. Focus on practical applicability for the target audience.
