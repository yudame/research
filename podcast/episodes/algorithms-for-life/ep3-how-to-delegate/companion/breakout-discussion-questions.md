# Breakout Discussion Questions: How to Delegate
## For Groups of 4 Business Owners (20-25 minutes)

**Audience:** Small business owners — restaurants, real estate, services, media, retail, trades
**Format:** One person reads the question aloud, everyone gets 1-2 minutes to respond before open discussion.

---

### 1. The Last Bad Hire

> Think about the last person you hired or promoted who didn't work out. Looking back honestly — was the failure about their technical skills, or about their attitude, coachability, or how they handled pressure?

---

### 2. The Interview Question

> Try the recommended interivew question on yourself now: "Describe a mistake you made at work and what you did about it." How is your "learning agility? Do you take ownership, deflect, or extract a lesson?

---

### 3. Your Delegation Ceiling

> What's one task you know you should hand off but can't bring yourself to let go of? What would it take for you to actually delegate it?

---

### 4. Who Learns Fastest

> Think about your current team. Who's the person who learns fastest from mistakes — not the most experienced or the smartest, but the one who bounces back and adjusts? How much responsibility do they have compared to others?

## Facilitator Notes

- **Group size:** 4 people per table is ideal. Large enough for diverse perspectives, small enough that everyone speaks.
- **Time management:** 2 minutes per person per question, plus 1-2 minutes of open riff. All 4 questions fits in 15-20 minutes.
- **Debrief option:** After breakout, ask 2-3 groups to share their most interesting insight with the full room. Focus on surprises — "What did you hear that you hadn't thought of before?"
