The Decision Flow
1 Screen the Person
Does this person learn from mistakes? Are they coachable? Learning agility correlates with success at 0.74 — stronger than height predicts weight.
2 Calibrate the Stakes
Low-risk: 50% competence threshold. Standard: 70%. High-stakes/irreversible: 90%.
3 Match the Style
Ask: “Would detailed direction or developing your own approach be more helpful?”
The Interview Cheat Code
Ask This Watch For
“Tell me about a time you had to learn something completely new. Walk me through your process.” Green: Extracts patterns, builds systems
Red: Focuses on “what” not “how”
“What’s the harshest feedback you’ve ever received, and what did you do with it?” Green: Ownership, specific change
Red: Deflection, blame-shifting
The Live Test: Give genuine feedback on their answer mid-interview. Green: Curiosity, adjustment
Red: Defensiveness, dismissiveness

Three Takeaways
1. Who > What
Who you delegate to matters more than which framework you use.
2. Tools, Not Rules
The 70% Rule, RACI, SLII have zero empirical validation. Use as thinking aids, not gospel.
3. Context Is King
More autonomy can backfire. Match your style to the person, culture, and stakes.
