# Research Brief: How to Delegate

## Topic
Effective delegation strategies and frameworks for leaders, founders, and managers.

## Episode Context
This is Episode 3 of the "Algorithms for Life" series, which explores research-backed frameworks for common life and work challenges. Previous episodes have covered decision-making and time management.

## Research Objectives

### Primary Questions
1. **What does empirical research say about delegation effectiveness?**
   - What predicts successful delegation outcomes?
   - What are the most common delegation failure modes?
   - How does delegation impact organizational performance?

2. **What psychological barriers prevent effective delegation?**
   - Identity attachment and control issues
   - Perfectionism and trust dynamics
   - "I can do it faster myself" fallacy

3. **What are evidence-based delegation frameworks?**
   - Knowledge transfer methodologies (tacit vs explicit knowledge)
   - Situational leadership models
   - Decision-making authority structures
   - Hiring for delegation success (learning agility, coachability)

4. **How has delegation changed with modern work contexts?**
   - Remote/hybrid work implications
   - AI and automation as delegation targets
   - Founder mode vs traditional management advice

### Specific Research Targets

**Meta-analyses and systematic reviews preferred:**
- Learning agility and delegation success correlations
- New hire failure rates and root causes
- Knowledge transfer effectiveness studies
- Leadership style matching to development levels

**Frameworks to investigate:**
- OPPTY framework (Observation, Practice, Partnering, Taking Responsibility, You're on your own)
- Situational Leadership II (D1-D4 development levels, S1-S4 leadership styles)
- RACI matrices and decision boundaries
- Founder Mode debate (Brian Chesky/Paul Graham 2024)

**Specific data points:**
- Opportunity cost calculations for founder time
- "70% rule" origins and empirical validation (if any)
- Remote work impact on delegation effectiveness
- AI delegation failure rates

### Quality Standards
- Prioritize peer-reviewed research over anecdotal advice
- Distinguish correlation from causation
- Report effect sizes where available
- Note study populations and generalizability
- Identify contradictory findings
- Include recent research (2020-2025) on AI/remote work impacts

## Output Requirements
Each research tool should provide:
1. Key empirical findings with citations
2. Practical frameworks grounded in research
3. Specific data points (percentages, correlations, effect sizes)
4. Source links for further investigation
5. Contradictory evidence or limitations
