## Counterpoint Opportunities

Based on research, these topics have natural tension for dialogue:

1. **Founder Mode vs. Traditional Delegation**
   - Speaker A: Chesky's success validates hands-on involvement
   - Speaker B: But what about survivorship bias? Selection effects?

2. **70% Rule**
   - Speaker A: Practical heuristic that enables action
   - Speaker B: Zero empirical validation - could be dangerous oversimplification

3. **AI Delegation**
   - Speaker A: Cost advantage ($20-200/mo vs $2000-5000/mo) makes it inevitable
   - Speaker B: 40%+ failure rates suggest premature adoption

4. **Learning Agility vs. Experience**
   - Speaker A: ρ=0.74 correlation is massive - prioritize adaptability
   - Speaker B: But can we reliably measure learning agility in interviews?
