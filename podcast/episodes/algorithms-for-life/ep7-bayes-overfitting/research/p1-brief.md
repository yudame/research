# Episode 7: Thinking Clearly

## Research Prompt

Research Bayes's Rule and overfitting as complementary frameworks for clearer thinking. For Bayes's Rule: investigate how Bayesian updating — starting with prior beliefs and revising them with new evidence — applies to medical diagnosis, hiring decisions, evaluating news, and everyday predictions. Explore base rate neglect (why people ignore priors), the prosecutor's fallacy, and how Bayesian reasoning improves calibration. Examine practical techniques for becoming a better Bayesian thinker. For overfitting: research how the machine learning concept of overfitting — where models learn noise instead of signal — maps to human decision-making. Investigate when thinking too hard, gathering too much data, or building too complex a mental model actually makes predictions worse. Explore the bias-variance tradeoff, regularization, cross-validation, and Occam's razor as principles for knowing when simplicity wins. Examine how the two connect: Bayes tells you how to update beliefs, while overfitting warns you when to stop — together they define the boundaries of good thinking.
