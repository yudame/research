# Episode 4: How to Communicate

## Research Prompt

Research how computer networking protocols — TCP/IP, packet switching, congestion control, routing algorithms, and buffering — provide models for human communication and social coordination. Explore how data moves through networks, what causes congestion and packet loss, and what protocols like TCP's flow control, exponential backoff, and acknowledgment systems teach us about managing conversations, information flow, and group communication. Investigate the parallels between network topology (hub-and-spoke, mesh, peer-to-peer) and social network structures. Examine what bandwidth limitations, latency, and the end-to-end principle reveal about effective communication strategies in work and life.
