# Episode 6: Letting Go

## Research Prompt

Research two counterintuitive problem-solving strategies from computer science: relaxation and randomness. For relaxation: investigate how mathematicians and computer scientists solve impossibly hard (NP-hard) problems by first loosening constraints — solving an easier version, then tightening back toward reality. Explore how linear programming relaxation, Lagrangian relaxation, and constraint relaxation apply to life decisions like career planning, scheduling, and negotiation (start with what's ideal if nothing were impossible, then reintroduce constraints). For randomness: examine when random or semi-random approaches outperform careful deliberation — from randomized algorithms and Monte Carlo methods to simulated annealing (accepting worse outcomes temporarily to escape local optima). Investigate how introducing randomness into decision-making can break analysis paralysis, overcome local optima in life choices, and why "good enough" random sampling often beats exhaustive search. Explore the connections between the two: both involve letting go of rigid optimization to find better solutions.
