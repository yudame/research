## Episode Description

Why do the same minds that generate breakthrough strategic vision struggle with sustained execution? This episode explores the paradox that high-openness, novelty-seeking cognitive architecture systematically undermines long-horizon follow-through—not as a character flaw, but as neurobiology operating as designed. Drawing from a 2024 meta-analysis showing human-AI collaboration underperforms on average (g = -0.23) but reverses for creative tasks, plus research on implementation intentions (d = 0.65), effectuation theory, and the constraint-creativity curve, we examine practical protocols for strategic selection under opportunity abundance.

**Full Report:** https://research.yuda.me/podcast/episodes/algorithms-for-life/ep2-strategic-selection/report.md

## NotebookLM Focus Prompt

Generate an engaging podcast discussion exploring the paradox of possibility-oriented minds struggling with strategic execution.

**Key Themes to Explore:**
1. The counterintuitive finding that visionary cognitive strengths create systematic execution liabilities
2. Human-AI collaboration research showing average underperformance but creative task reversal
3. The Big Five tension between Openness and Conscientiousness in entrepreneurs
4. Implementation intentions as highest-leverage intervention (d = 0.65)
5. Effectuation theory: how expert entrepreneurs actually make portfolio decisions
6. The constraint-creativity inverted U-curve
7. Practical protocols: minimum viable structure, 2-3 project limit, review cadences

**Tone:** Intellectually rigorous but accessible. Treat this as helping high-potential individuals understand their own cognitive architecture and design systems that work with (not against) their traits.

**Avoid:** Generic productivity advice, shame-based framing, oversimplifying the research
