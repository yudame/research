# Episode 2: Strategic Selection - Decision Algorithms for High-Possibility Minds

## Research Prompt

Research the psychology and decision science of strategic selection under conditions of opportunity abundance, examining why visionary, possibility-oriented thinkers (exemplified by ENFP-type cognition) struggle with long-horizon strategy execution despite strong creative output. Investigate evidence-based frameworks for initiative comparison, prioritization, and sustained execution—including how AI tools might extend strategic capability rather than constrain it.

**Core questions to explore:**

### The Opportunity Overload Problem
- What does cognitive psychology reveal about decision-making when facing multiple high-value, long-horizon opportunities (6-18 month initiatives)?
- How does opportunity cost function when comparing initiatives with different time horizons, risk profiles, and resource requirements?
- What research exists on "too many parallel tracks" as a failure mode? How does cognitive load from context-switching affect strategic execution?
- What does the research show about premature commitment versus analysis paralysis? Where is the optimal decision point?
- What are "kill criteria" and why do some personality types struggle to abandon initiatives? What does sunk cost research reveal?

### Personality and Executive Function
- What does personality psychology (Big Five, MBTI research validity, temperament studies) reveal about individual differences in strategic planning versus idea generation?
- How do high-openness, high-extraversion individuals differ in executive function, sustained attention, and delayed gratification?
- What does research show about novelty-seeking as both strength (innovation) and liability (abandonment)?
- How does intrinsic motivation research apply to long-term strategic execution? What kills motivation for possibility-oriented thinkers?
- What cognitive strengths do visionary thinkers bring that structured systems should preserve rather than suppress?

### Decision Frameworks and Trade-off Legibility
- What frameworks exist for comparing 2-5 major initiatives with incommensurable benefits?
- How can long-term trade-offs be made "legible" (visible, concrete, emotionally salient) to support better decisions?
- What does research show about decision matrices, weighted scoring, and their actual effectiveness versus intuitive judgment?
- How do expert strategists and successful entrepreneurs make portfolio decisions across multiple opportunities?
- What role does external accountability play in decision quality and follow-through?

### Minimum Viable Structure
- What is the minimum scaffolding that increases follow-through without crushing intrinsic motivation?
- How do different levels of structure affect creative output and sustained execution?
- What does habit formation research suggest about building strategic review rhythms?
- How do milestone systems and progress tracking affect motivation for different personality types?
- What's the evidence on planning granularity—when does detailed planning help versus hinder?

### AI as Cognitive Extension
- How can AI tools extend strategic decision-making capability rather than replace human judgment?
- What AI-assisted workflows exist for initiative comparison and prioritization?
- How might AI help maintain awareness of trade-offs, opportunity costs, and long-term consequences?
- What role can AI play in generating execution scaffolds that reduce abandonment?
- How should AI recommendations balance "pick one and focus" versus "do more with better coordination"?
- What are the risks of AI-assisted strategic planning (over-reliance, loss of intuition, false precision)?

### Execution and Follow-Through
- What does research show about why strategic plans fail at the execution phase?
- How do implementation intentions and "if-then" planning affect follow-through rates?
- What role does environmental design play in sustained strategic execution?
- How do accountability structures (coaches, peers, public commitments) affect completion rates?
- What's the evidence on iterative planning versus upfront comprehensive planning?

**Research methodology:**
- Prioritize peer-reviewed research in decision science, cognitive psychology, personality psychology, and organizational behavior
- Examine meta-analyses on decision-making, planning, and goal achievement
- Distinguish laboratory findings from real-world strategic contexts
- Report effect sizes and practical significance
- Include research on entrepreneurship, innovation management, and strategic planning
- Examine both individual psychology and organizational/systems approaches
- Consider research on ADHD and executive function as relevant to understanding attention and follow-through challenges
- Look for contradictory findings and areas of ongoing debate
- Include practitioner perspectives from strategy consultants, executive coaches, and successful multi-project leaders

## Context

ENFP leaders often generate many compelling, high-variance ideas. The failure mode isn't creativity—it's selection, sequencing, and sustained execution when multiple 6-18 month initiatives compete for attention and organizational resources. The goal is to design an AI-assisted decision + planning system that preserves ENFP strengths (vision, people energy, possibility) while adding INTJ-like structure (trade-offs, prioritization, milestones, risk management).

The key insight: ENFPs want to figure out how to do more with the help of AI, not necessarily limit what they can do. The solution should empower while prioritizing—both expanding capability and maintaining focus.
